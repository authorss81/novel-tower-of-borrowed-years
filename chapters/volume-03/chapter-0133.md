# Chapter 133 — I Was Not in the Room

The nine rows on the shoulder above the new drain went into a shock on the twelfth of the harvest month and the shock was still standing on the sixteenth, because a shoulder with a fresh drain cut into the top of it will not carry a cart, and everybody on the second terrace knew that, and there were nine rows of barley on it that had come up on ground that had no business carrying them and were standing in a shock four feet high waiting for somebody to work out how to get them off.

That was the work on the second terrace on the morning of the sixteenth. It was not the ninth line of anything and it was on nobody's list. Six men were lifting sheaves off the shock onto their shoulders and carrying them down to the headland, and two men were in the drain with a bar, and nine people were doing the two jobs in a lane on a terrace four miles and eleven switchbacks above a stone bridge.

Marek Vale came into the yard at about the second bell and Zofia Teran was in it with a rope and a child's coat over the rail, and she put the child's coat on the rail because it was in the way, which is what she has done with that coat three times now and has never once done anything else for anybody.

"You look like a man who has walked from my gate to a threshing floor and back and has not sat down, and you have not, and I am not going to help you up a road," she said. "Say what you have come to say, and say it to me, because I am the one who has been to the doors, and then I am going to tell you something and you are not going to enjoy it."

---

He got four lines in.

"There is a paper and it names a class and the class is the seed-keepers of the four terraces after this season—"

"That is the part you have already got," said Zofia Teran, "and you have come four miles to bring me the part you have got, and I heard about it on the day you went past, from a woman on the first terrace, or I have been told about it, or somebody has told me about it, and I do not know which and I have not asked, and you are going to start at the other end.

"Start at the other end. What do you want. Not what is on the paper. What do you want."

Marek said the other end, and it took him about a minute, and when he had finished she took the rope off the rail and put it down.

---

"First," she said. "I am not writing it down."

"I was going to ask you to."

"You were going to ask me to and I have been waiting two days for you to get to it, because it is the only thing in the next four minutes that is actually important." She went inside and got the exercise book off the shelf inside the door and held it without opening it. "This has no name at the head of it and no name at the foot of it and it lives in an apron, and I have told you in this yard what it is and I am not going to tell you again, and I am not writing your nine words in it.

"And the reason is not the one you are thinking of. You are thinking it is because a paper outlives the people on it. It does, and you are right, and Tomas Vale says it better than I could and I have never met him.

"The reason is that the day those nine words are in a book in this valley, somebody in nine months is going to stand in a room and say *the second terrace said the nine words*. And there will be a piece of paper on the table with the day on it, and there will be a woman who kept it, and it will be true that I said them and true that I wrote them and true that you asked me to, and it will be a thing that happened, and it will settle, and the second terrace will have said the nine words in a book for ever. And then there will be no telling anybody anything on this terrace again, ever, by anybody, because the one time we did it we put it in a book."

She put the book back on the shelf and shut it with her hand.

"You are going to have to say it in your mouth," said Zofia Teran. "That is where it has to live. I am sorry. It is a poor place for a thing and it does not last and I have watched a sentence like that last about four days in this valley, and there is nothing I have that will do it better and I have thought about it for two days."

---

"Second," she said. "And this is the part you are not going to enjoy, and I have had it since you went past my gate two days ago, and I want it said on a working day with six men on my shoulder and not on a day when anybody could say I was dramatic about it.

"The ninth of the growth month. The third bell. A lamp you did not light."

"I know what day it was."

"You do not know what it was, and I am going to tell you, and then I am not going to discuss it, and this is not me refusing to be reasonable, this is me refusing to be reasonable.

"You came up this road at the third bell before light with a lamp you did not light and you spent a season of this basin's out of your own hands on nine rows on a shoulder above a drain, and the ground under those rows is a woman of thirty-one's and it is held in her mother's name, and you did not ask either of them, and one of them does not know it happened at all.

"And nine people on this terrace worked that out in four days without needing you to explain one word of it, and they did not work it out because of anything you said. They worked it out because nine rows came up on ground that has never grown nine rows at once in the time this terrace has been here, and the drain above them was cut in the third month, and a woman with a book walked round every one of them and wrote down the day.

"And I did not come down your road. That was yours to do and you have not done it, and there is a difference between a man not coming down a road and a woman not coming down a road, and I have thought about which of us was owed it and I have not changed my mind and I am not going to."

---

Marek Vale stood in a yard on the second terrace and had two answers and used the wrong one first.

"I can say I am sorry—"

"No," said Zofia Teran. "You cannot, and I would not let you, and if you said it I would have to spend the rest of the morning deciding whether to believe you and I have a shoulder to get off."

She put her hands flat on the rail, on the child's coat.

"Third, and then I have said it. I am not going to be the woman who came down the hill."

"You have said that in this yard before."

"I said it in the third month and I meant it about a season of weather and a paper a man in a good coat left on a bench in Bell Stair," said Zofia Teran. "And I have not taken it back and I am not taking it back now, and I am going to be that woman about this as well, and there is no paper in this kingdom in which a woman of forty-four on a terrace can be told she has been promoted."

She came off the rail and stood in front of him.

"You want me to be named. Nine words of yours and a class, and a household, and a ground. And the ground you want is not mine, and it is not anybody's in this yard. It is on a board in a loft four miles up that road and it is held in the name of a woman of fifty-eight, and the store it stands on is the only store on this terrace with a lock on it, and a store with a lock has a holder, and a holder is a person, and a person can be asked.

"And you have not asked me who that person is, and I am going to tell you, and it is not me, and it is the mother of the woman whose ground you put it in, and she is fifty-eight and she does not count in this valley, and I have been to her door.

"So the person you have to ask is her. And you are not going to ask her, because you have worked out by now that asking her is asking the woman, and asking the woman is asking me in front of nine people. And if you did ask, she would say what I am going to say, and she would say it better, and she would not have walked four miles to work it out.

"And I have been the woman somebody comes down the hill to since the ninth month of last year, and there are nine households on this terrace who have been named in a yard by somebody with a book in it, and not one of them has been named by me."

---

And then she said the sentence, and she said it flat, and it was two sentences and they were the plainest thing said in the Red Uplands that month.

"I was not in the room. You were in a ditch."

Nothing happened on the shoulder. A man carried a sheaf past them and put it down and picked up another one.

"That is it," said Zofia Teran. "That is the whole of the ground and you are not going to be able to argue with it, and I would like you to hear that I know that, and I would like you to hear that I have thought about how you would answer it and I have not found the answer and it does not matter if you have.

"You are asking me to be named for a thing you did in a ditch without asking anybody, by a man who has been in this yard twice in four days and who told me two days ago that he cannot tell me what it will land on. And in nine weeks' time, or in nine months, or in the fourth month of next year, a rate or a figure or a notice is going to arrive on this terrace that is the cost of it, and I am going to have to stand here and look at it, and I will be able to say that I said no to a man on a working day while six men were on my shoulder, and that is worth something, and it is the only thing I have been able to get for myself in a year, and you have come and taken it."

She put the child's coat off the rail and folded it over her arm.

"No," she said. "We are not going to be the people who sign for the next one. Not this season and not the next. And I am not going to tell you why in a nicer way than that, because you will take the nicer way up the road and I do not want the nicer way in your mouth."

---

They cut the shock off the shoulder that afternoon.

That is the rest of the sixteenth of the harvest month and there is not much in it. The shoulder would not take a cart and they carried the sheaves down on shoulders to the headland in about nine trips an hour for about nine hours, and the men in the drain cleared the cut so that the water from the headland had somewhere to go, and at about the sixth bell a cart came up the lane from the shared floor and took the sheaves off the headland two at a time and went down, and the whole of it went on because the barley is barley and it does not know what was said in a yard.

Stana Peric was on the headland for the whole of it with a child on her hip and she did not say one word to anybody about the yard, and she has never said one word about the yard, and there is nothing for her to say, because the season that came off that shoulder was given to her in the growth month and she has been putting her seed in and shutting the door on it ever since and has not asked why and is not going to.

Nobody in that yard said the word *failure*, and nobody thanked Marek Vale for coming, and Zofia Teran read the old season out of the board in the loft at about the eighth bell for the man who cannot read, as she does on a day she has told him about, and Marek Vale was not asked to stay and did not.

He went down eleven switchbacks that night with a sentence he could not put anywhere, and on the way he worked out that he had now been told yes once and no once in three days, on two terraces, by two women who have never met, and that he had gained nothing by either answer, and that the sentence he came up here with was shorter than it was when he came in the gate, and that the sentence he would take down would be shorter still, and that there was no form in this kingdom in which a sentence gets longer by being refused.
