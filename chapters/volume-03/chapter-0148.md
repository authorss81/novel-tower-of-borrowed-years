# Chapter 148 — The Levy Tin

The levy came round on the second of the fourth month of MR 415 and it came round the way it has come round since the seed month of MR 414, which is that there is a tin on the second trestle from the first bell and Sanne Droll sits behind it and nobody is asked to give anything, and a person gives it or does not, and Droll says the amount out loud once to each person because she is asked the four questions like anybody else and the amount is part of that.

It is a hundred and forty a season. It has been a hundred and forty a season since the seed month and it was a hundred and forty a season in the year before that, and the figure is in a notice in Sedge's hand on the wall of the shed, and there is no form in this kingdom on which anybody can be produced to say where the figure came from or who set it, and the yard does not know and has never been told.

The tin went into the office at the fourth bell and it was not counted out in the yard.

That is the whole of the arrangement and there are about nine people in Tallow Ward who know it is deliberate and all of them found out by accident.

"There is no total," said Aldis Kern, from the step, to a man who had come down the lane on the second of the fourth month to ask what the ward took in a season.

"Then I will add it up myself over a year and come back in a year and ask you again."

"You can do that," said Kern. "There is a hundred and forty in a notice and it has been a hundred and forty for a year and it will be a hundred and forty next year, and if you add up the tin yourself over a year you will get a number, and I hope you enjoy having it, and I am not going to give it to you and neither is anybody else in this yard."

---

The reason is in the yard's own method and it has been in the yard's own method since the flood year and it is not printed anywhere and no office in this kingdom has ever been shown it.

A total is a figure a body puts on itself in a room on a day in the words of the body. A figure that appears on a board the same way twice turns the board into a roll, and a roll is a thing a man in a good coat can produce at anybody in nine months. A hundred and forty in a notice is a figure a body has put on itself in a room on a day, and it is there for anybody to read, and a person in this lane can walk in and read it off the wall in about four seconds, and that is the whole of what the notice is for.

A total on the tin would be different. A total on the tin is a figure that somebody in this yard put on something in this yard, and it would be a figure about a person who gave and a person who did not, and the only way to make it is to write a figure next to a name, and this yard has no form on which a name may be written next to a figure, and has never had one, and the reason it has never had one is that the register's four questions are four questions about a paper and a person, and a tin is neither, and the day a tin acquires a column it is a receipt, and a receipt is a thing a person is handed, and a person who has been handed a receipt has been told a figure and can be produced with it.

So the tin goes into the office and the office has a box and the box is not counted out in the lane, and there is no total, and the yard does not know what the yard took, and neither does the man who came down the lane on the second of the fourth month with a pencil.

---

There were about seventy people in the lane at the second bell and about twenty in the shed, which holds twenty-two, and Teodor Lask was reading somebody's second line out loud with his back to a bench frame, twice, at the ordinary speed, and the room did not talk over a line.

The wall list had about forty lines on it. The right-hand end of the fourth column had nothing at the head of it and has had nothing at the head of it since the second week, and nineteen hands declined to name it in the first fortnight and about four hundred yards of the lane voted in four minutes not to fill it and not to rule it out.

The fifth line of the yard's form is the hours and it is empty, and the yard has refused it twice on its own rule about a round that repeats, and the sixth line is the days and it is empty and always will be, because there is no box in this kingdom for the days.

The register has been open on the days the rota says since the eighteenth of the ninth month of MR 413 and the flat board with four ruled sheets is under the trestle and has not been out, and the certified true duplicate of the seasonal form is in a flat case under the same trestle and has not been out either, and neither of them is evidence of anything, and there is no room in this basin in which either of them could be produced.

And the yard's water still does not come down the Ropewalk standpipe on a queue morning, which it did not on the morning of the second of the fourth month, and the woman who carries it now comes down at the second bell to beat the queue, and that is where the whole of the cost of the queue is, and it is on one woman's back, and it has been on one woman's back since the seed month, and her name is not in this chapter because she has not asked for it to be anywhere.

---

Ondra Kelb came down the lane at about the third bell with a satchel and a good coat on, because he is a clerk whom other people ask things of now, and he has not been asked about a hold that is on the books at Verrow and which nobody in this basin can see, and his left hand is still a nine-year-old's and is not his and did not stop being that in the growth month.

He stood in front of the wall list for about a minute and then said:

"There is a column at the end of the fourth with nothing at the head of it, and I have been in about nine offices in three years and every one of them has a head on every column, and I have been trying since the seed month to work out how a column can be in a room for a year with nothing at the head of it and not be a mistake."

"It is not a mistake," said Droll.

"Then what is it."

"It is a column that nineteen people declined to name and about four hundred yards of this lane voted in four minutes not to fill and not to rule out," said Sanne Droll, "and if you put a head on it then you have made it a column, and if you have made it a column then a man in a good coat can produce this wall in nine months and say that a ward's counter has a column called *such and such*, and there are about four hundred people in this ward and the reason there is nothing at the head of it is that not one of the four hundred of them is that thing."

"There is a right answer," said Kelb. "I have been in nine offices. Somebody in this room knows what the right answer is and if nobody is going to say it then the register is going to be a room where a person can ask what a piece of paper is and it is going to be a place where nothing can be found, and those are not the same thing and one of them is worse than the other."

"Nobody knows," said Aldis Kern from the step. "I have been on that step since the flood year and I know, and I will tell you what I know, and it is that I do not know, and that is a different thing from a man in a good coat's *such and such*, and it is the only head this column has ever had and you are not going to be able to write it down."

"Then the column stays bare."

"The column stays bare," said Sanne Droll, "and you are not going to be thanked for asking and I am not going to be thanked for answering and nobody in this lane is going to be thanked for either, and that is not a policy. It is a yard."

---

About nine people stopped coming in the first year of that register, and about nine more stopped coming in the fourth month of MR 415, and there is no way in this kingdom of telling the two lots apart, and nobody has ever tried, and the second lot is not smaller than the first and the yard is not bigger.

Nobody in that yard has any idea which nine, in either year. Droll thinks it is the people who come at the second bell, because the second bell got worse when the bench moved into the shed on the days the rota said and there is a queue in the lane at the second bell now and there was not one at the second bell before. Lask thinks it is a woman of about sixty who has not come since the growth month and who was the only person who had ever brought anything nobody else had brought. A man from the water office came down the lane on the third of the third month and counted the heads in the yard at four bells for four days running, on his own initiative, without being asked by anybody, and got a number, and told nobody, and has not been back.

Milan Vray has the opposite number and will not give it to anybody and has not been asked for it twice.

"The yard has cost this ward its water and about nine people and nobody knows which nine," said Aldis Kern, "and I have been asked by two men in good coats in a year whether this room is a success and I have said no to both of them and I said no to the second one in a worse temper than the first, and I would like it noticed that a register is not a seed and that nobody in this yard has ever said it was one, and that I have heard the word said in a lane about twice and both times by men who had not been in the shed."

---

The district's rate came on in the same month.

That is the plain fact of the second of the fourth month of MR 415 and there is no office in this basin that sends anybody to tell a district it is short, and the low ground's second sowing did not come, and the carts turned round on the ninth of the harvest month, and about a fifth of the first did not come either.

A district that has come in short does not pay. A district answers a shortfall with a rate, and a rate goes on a person with a name, because a charge has to have somebody on it or it is not a charge, it is a note, and a roll is a sheet of paper with a roll number on it, and a roll number is a number, and a number about a district cannot be told anything and cannot be asked anything and cannot be in a room.

The rate is on the low ground. It has been entered in the fourth month. It is on about nine names, and every one of the nine is a person, and none of the nine is a household and none of the nine is a terrace and none of the nine has been asked anything at a counter in this basin, and there is a piece of paper in a drawer in Rainmarket with eleven lines on it and nothing at the head of it which is a different size of the same shape entirely and cannot be put beside the first one on any form in this kingdom.

Mirren Fisk went to eleven kitchens in the fourth month and said a number out loud in each of them, as a person who has been in that kitchen, and she was told no by four of them and did not argue with any of the four, and she has told that figure to nobody outside the eleven kitchens and there is no form on which she could be produced at anybody in nine months.

And the register sat there all month with the tin on the second trestle and no total in it, and the wall list gained three lines and lost none, and the column at the end of the fourth had nothing at the head of it at the fourth bell on the last day of the month, and the fifth line was empty, and the sixth line was empty, and the notice in Sedge's hand was still on the wall with a hundred and forty in it, and Lask read somebody's fourth line out twice at the ordinary speed and the room did not talk over it, and nobody in that shed was thanked for anything by anybody, then or afterwards, and the register is a practice and not a remedy and there is no office in this kingdom that has been told it is either.
