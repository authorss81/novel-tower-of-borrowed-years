# Chapter 188 — A Challenge Against the Room

There is no form in this kingdom on which a challenge may be refused, and on the twenty-fifth of the fourth month of MR 416 a man in a good coat brought one to the second of the four rooms behind the Threshold, which is ten feet by eight and is the room a challenge is heard in, and he was not refused, and nobody in the corridor could have made him be refused, and he knew that before he came.

Deputy Vera Tolek, forty-four, nineteen years a hearing officer and two years in that room, let him stand at the rail for four seconds before she said anything, and then she counted his sentences out loud, the way she does, because counting them is the only thing she has ever found that slows a person down.

"You have said four," she said. "One of them was a question and I am going to deal with the four and not with the question, and I am going to tell you before I start that I am not going to refuse you, and I have never refused one, and I am not going to start on a man in a good coat in the fourth month."

---

He gave his name and it was not a name any of the twenty-one people in the corridor would have recognised, and he did not offer it, and she entered it. He said he was employed. He said his business was to ask. He said the notice had gone up on the low-ground road on the twenty-second of this month and had four things on it in the ordinary language of a form and that the fourth of the four said that any person might come to a counter on the causeway side and ask whether their attestation had been produced, and the answer would be given, and nobody was to be charged, and there was no form on which a person might be refused.

"That is a true copy of the notice," said Deputy Tolek. "Go on. That is three."

"And I want this court to find," said the man in the good coat, "that the room the notice is copied from is a room with no office and no responsible party and no standing of any kind, and that a thing said in it is said by nobody and binds nobody, and that it is not a source anybody may be instructed by, and I want that entered and I want it dated, and I have brought it as a challenge and I know that a challenge is free and that there is no form on which you may refuse it."

"That is four," said Deputy Tolek, "and I am going to say the thing I have to say before anything else, and then I am going to say it once.

"The notice was not copied from the room."

---

Nobody in that room said anything for a moment, and it was the man in the good coat who moved first, which Deputy Tolek noted and did not remark on.

"Read it again," he said.

"The notice was not copied from the room. The room is over a dye house in a lane that has no name on any map a clerk in this city can look at, and it is about nine feet by eleven, and it has a rail round three sides of it made out of a bed frame and three bought brackets and one shoe-tongue of iron made in a lane. It is kept by a woman of thirty-four who has been at a door for nine years asking people one question and has never written one answer down. It has no office, no employer, no surety, no seal, no minute, no stipend, no membership and no name on a door, and it is not a court and will not be called one.

"The notice came from a table of five in a building in Bell Stair, on the twenty-second of the fourth month of this year, on a minute with a day on it, and it was asked for by a woman of fifty-one who has been at the tower's public witness counter on the causeway side for thirty years, and that woman has never been to that room and has never heard of it, and nobody at that table knew there was a room, and the woman at the counter did not mention one and would not have known what to call it.

"You have put two things in front of me and they are the same four sentences and they have never been in the same room, and that is the whole of my finding and I am going to come back to what that costs you in about nine minutes."

---

"You are a man in a good coat at a window with a list," said Marek Vale, from the side of the room with no rail, where a man stands when he is not wanted to be looked at.

He should not have spoken. He said so himself, twice, in the next four minutes, and Deputy Tolek let him and entered that he had spoken and did not enter what he had said.

"You are a man in a good coat at a window with a list at the eighth bell," he said. "And there have been four of you in this basin in five months and you have never met each other and none of you has been asked what you are and I would like you to notice that nobody in this room is going to ask you either, including me, and I have been in this basin four months and I know exactly why."

"I am not here to be asked what I am," said the man in the good coat. "I am here about the room."

"Then you are here about the room."

---

Deputy Tolek read out what she could enter and said what she could not enter, and she did it in nine sentences and she did not hurry any of them.

"I can enter that a notice was ordered by a table of five on the twenty-second of the fourth month of this year and was put up in three places, and that it says four things, and that the fourth of them is true. I can enter that a room exists which was not the source of it, and that neither the room nor the table knew of the other, and that there is no instrument in this kingdom by which one of them could have known.

"I cannot find that the room has standing. A challenge requires a sheet, there is no sheet, and the object of yours is a requirement, and I have had a requirement in this room in the third month of this year and I entered a finding about it in a book and it is a quarter of a page and it says that an instrument cannot do what it is for, and it is true, and it is worth nothing, and I have been asked by a man in a good coat in the last fortnight whether it is going to be produced against him and I said it might be.

"I cannot say a room is right or wrong. There is no such finding available to me and there is a finding of the fourth of the first month in a book in this building that says this court cannot enter a finding that any person did or did not consent to anything, and it has looked and there is not one, and I am not going to invent a finding to fill the place of one.

"And I am not going to refuse you, and I have not, and I will not, and I would like it noticed that I have said so twice and that the second time cost me more than the first."

---

"Find it against me," said the man in the good coat. "Find the whole thing against me. I do not care what it says."

"That is the first sentence anybody has said in this room in nineteen years that I could have used and you have not got it." Deputy Tolek put her pen down. "You have not got it because you have not brought a sheet, and you have not brought a sheet because you do not want a finding, you want a prohibition, and a prohibition is a policy, and a hearing officer cannot make a policy and has never pretended to.

"And I am going to say the part that is mine and not the court's, and I am going to say it because you have been fair and I have noticed and I do not reward fairness, I only record it.

"You are right."

He put his head slightly on one side, which Deputy Tolek also noted and did not remark on.

"Two true things happened in this city in this month and they happened a long way apart and neither of them knows about the other, and a notice on a wall that tells nine hundred people to go to a counter and ask a question is an instruction to every person in this basin to distrust you, and your work is a queue at a window with a list and it depends on the opposite. I know that because I have been a hearing officer for nineteen years and I have had men in good coats at my door asking me to be careful about what I write down, and I have written it down, and I would have written it down anyway, and I am telling you that I would have.

"And there is no office in this kingdom that can stop a man in a good coat at a window, and there is no form on which a person may be told to stop him, and the table of five that paid for your notice has an empty seat and has had one seven times and this was the eighth, and the person who would have stopped you would have to be a person with a name at the top of something, and there is not one of those in this basin either."

"So it stays up," he said.

"It stays up," said Deputy Tolek, "and I have entered a finding that says where it came from, and in four years you can produce that finding at a clerk in a good coat and it will say that a table of five and a room that is not an office said the same four true things and had never heard of each other, and there will be nothing whatever in the page to say what either of us thought about it, because I did not think about it and I am not a person who thinks about things in a room, I enter them."

---

She read the finding out loud twice at the ordinary speed before she signed it, which is what a person does in a room that has learned anything at all, and then she said:

"One more, and it is short, and it is the last thing I am going to say today.

"There was a person present in this room at the rail of the side with no rail, from about the third bell, whom nobody addressed. That is a fact about this room and I am entering it because a court that leaves out the people in it is a court that is deciding in advance who was in it.

"Mr Vale has asked me twice not to enter it. I am entering it. I told him on the thirteenth of the third month that I was not going to be thanked for entering things and I have not been and I am not going to be now, and I have told him the reason twice and I am not going to tell it again in this building.

"He will be in the finding as a person who was in a room and was not spoken to. That is the whole of it and it is a true thing and it is about this room and not about him, and if anybody ever produces this page at him they may produce it, and I have entered it with a day on it on purpose."

The man in the good coat went out through a corridor with nine or ten other people in it and did not look at anybody, and nine or ten people who had come to see whether a challenge could be refused found out that it could not and that a hearing officer would not pretend a requirement was a sheet, and went home along a causeway with nothing to show for it except four things they had heard said in a small room by two people who were both right.
