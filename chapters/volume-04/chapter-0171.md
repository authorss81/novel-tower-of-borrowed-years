# Chapter 171 — The Tools Come Off the Bank

The sledge went off the top of the reach at about a quarter past the sixth bell on the nineteenth of the second month of MR 416, and it was not a decision anybody had made that morning, it was a decision the sledge made when the man behind it put his shoulder into it and the clay where the sledge had been standing all month gave up.

The clay did not go far. It went about nine feet and it came down the face in three pieces and the bottom piece folded once, the way a thing folds when it has been sat on for six weeks, and it went into the low ground and made a noise that four men heard and a fifth man did not.

The bank was a foot high at that end and about a mile and a bit long and the foot of it was the best clay in the basin, and it had been the best clay in the basin since a marshal put a bare hand flat on it and said so.

"You have lost about four barrows," said Seren Calder. She had not been on the reach. She had been standing at the top of it for about a quarter of an hour before the sledge went, in the same coat, and she had a list on a board, and she had been looking at the place where the face was beginning to sweat rather than at the eleven men.

"Nine," said Milos Havel, who keeps the district's tools and the key to the store they go back into, and who was not a clerk and had said so when he was asked. "That was nine barrows, and it went in before anybody could put a barrow under it, and I would not have bet on four."

---

"Do not touch the face," said Calder. "That is not a marshal's order, it is the only advice I have got left and I have got no standing to give an order about this bank in this room."

Nobody touched the face.

Havel got the men to move the sledges that were still on the top back about twenty feet, off the line the sweat had come up, and he did it in nine minutes and he did not raise his voice once, and then he stood at the top of the reach with his hands in his coat.

"I am not here on behalf of anybody who has authority over you," he said. "I have the key to the store and the store is on the low-ground road and everything in it belongs to the district, and if there is no works in force then the tools go back into the store tonight, because a tool that is out of a store is on a district and a tool that is in a store is on a store."

"Whose works is in force," said Calder.

"None, and I have got a board on a trestle up there that says otherwise, and I have been the man who keeps the tools for nine years and I have never once had to say the word *none* about a works board, and I would like it noticed that it is a minute past six and the copy room shut at six and I have not got anywhere to be."

"It is the six," said Marek Vale.

"It is the six," said Havel.

---

The board was on a trestle about forty feet back from the broken face and it had eleven names on it in a fair hand, and the eleven names were the eleven names of the witnesses, and the district had put eleven on because a works board takes eleven, and the number of people whose attestation was of record in this basin and had been produced was not eleven.

The district had been asked on the eighteenth and had not answered.

Kade had said on the sixteenth that a district which says nothing has voted, and had said it four days before anybody else thought of asking, and the district had then gone on not saying anything for a month, and there is no form on which a district may be entered as having declined, and there is no form on which it may be entered as having agreed either, and a silence is not a thing anybody can serve.

"Right," said Calder. "I am going to say the whole of this in the order I say it and I am not going to soften any of it, and then Milos is going to take the sledges off in an hour and I am not going to stop him.

"The clay is good. The clay has been good all month and it is good now. Eleven gangs have been on this reach since the first month and they have put up a foot of it and they have done it properly, and I have watched all of it, and none of that is going to be in trouble with me or with anybody.

"And a foot of it is going to be taken off in an hour and put in a store because a board with eleven names on it cannot be a works, and the reason it cannot be a works is that a witness has to be a person standing in a place on a day, and the people of record in this basin are nine, and the nine have been produced once, and there is no form on which a person may be produced twice.

"The ground has been going soft since the second of this month. It went off in the second month and the low ground goes under in the third. I have said the same words on this reach on the sixteenth of the first month and I am saying them again on the nineteenth of the second and neither of those two things is a figure I made up. It is what the water does. I have been doing this nineteen years and I have never once known it to do otherwise."

---

"And here is the part I have not said on a bank yet," she said, "and I have been waiting to say it because I wanted to be sure of it, and I am sure of it now because a man who keeps a key stood on that face and told me there was no works.

"The last time this ground was in this position, a rate came on it.

"A rate came on this ground in the fifth month of MR 415. It was going to come. It had been entered and it was going to come on about nine names, and one of the nine is a man called Petar Duss, and Duss is a haulier and he is not a witness to anything and has not been asked to be and is not going to be.

"And it did not come. Not because anybody cancelled it. Because in the third month of MR 415 a record loan was sealed at a records office a night and a day up this river, on a standing authority, correctly, by a man from the board above that office, and its scope names a line of district accounts, and this ground's account is on that line, and an account that is on that line does not get struck out at the end of a season.

"That is what a winter costs. That is the whole of what it costs. Eleven thousand pounds of clay did not fall out of the sky. Somebody paid for the winter and the price was entered in the last of five boxes on a piece of paper up that river and it is not a figure and it is not a number of months.

"It is fourteen attestations."

---

Nobody said anything on the reach for about the length of a line.

Marek Vale had said that number out loud on a bank on the sixteenth of the first month with three people in front of him, and he had said it before she asked, and he had said it because he had nothing else. He said it now, and he said it the same way, and the difference was that on the sixteenth there had been a bank under their feet and now there was a hole in one.

"The fourteen are in the same book as the nine," he said. "I have been up that river twice and both times I came down with nothing in my coat. I have got no paper and I have got no standing and I have not got an office, and on the thirtieth of the ninth month of last year I stood behind a comparison in a room eight feet by eleven and put my name in a box, and no room in this kingdom will take my word for anything for as long as that paper stands.

"So you have the figure. And you know what I am, because a settlement marshal spends four days in a basin and reads the files, and my file says the same thing on the back of a printed notice about a standpipe with a day on it that I asked her for and would have got anyway."

"I have read it," said Calder.

"I know you have. I am not complaining and I am not asking you to do anything with it. I am telling you the price of the figure on it."

---

"And now the thing I have not got," said Calder, "and I am going to say it in front of eleven men and a man with a key because I have been in five rooms in a month and every one of them has ended in a form and I am tired of it.

"I can seal a settlement on a surety. That is the instrument. That is the whole of the instrument, and it is short, and I have used it twice in nineteen years and both times I was right about the water.

"And a surety is a person, and a person is named, and the naming is done by an office out of a book. And there is a thing about that which I found in a drawer up that river a week ago and which I have not been able to put down since, and I am going to put it down here.

"A room in which instrument is witnessed is recognised by the region where a responsible party is named. A responsible party may be a person, an office, a share, a ward, a guild, a market, or a company. And where the responsible party is a person, the form requires an employer.

"So a room can be a person's, and then that room has to have an employer, and I have not got a person in this basin who is not a spent witness and a man with a key.

"A room can be an office's. There is no office in this basin whose business this is.

"A room can be a ward's. There is no ward here. I have asked twice and I was told there is no district and both men were correct.

"A room can be a share's. There is a share. I have been up the hill to the third room behind that hall and it is the share's room and it is a good room and a paper witnessed in it is good outside it, and I would seal a settlement on that room tomorrow.

"And I will not, and I am going to tell you why in one sentence, and then a man with a key is going to take four sledges off this reach.

"A share is members. A share has a minute, and a minute is a form, and a form is what I have spent a month getting away from. The day I seal an emergency settlement on a share's minute, I have made the share the responsible party for a public emergency, and in about four years a man in a good coat will produce that minute at a clerk in a good coat and the minute will say that the share agreed."

---

The first sledge went off the reach at about the seventh bell, and the second behind it, and the men took them down the lane at a walk because a sledge on a soft lane is a bad morning for whoever is behind it.

Havel went with them and came back up at about half past the seventh and stood where the sledges had been and looked at the hole in the face and did not say anything for a while, and then he said to Marek Vale, who was the only other person on the top of the reach with him:

"You will want to know whether I was right to come."

"You were right to come."

"I was not right to come and I knew it when I was coming and I came anyway, and that is the only part of it I am going to have to think about at night, and it is not going to get better by thinking about it, so I would rather you did not be kind about it."

He went down the lane after the sledges.

---

Calder did not go down the lane. She stood at the top of the reach with the list on the board against her knee and looked at nine feet of clay that had been standing for six weeks and was now in the low ground doing exactly what clay does.

"You were here on the twentieth of the first month," she said.

"I was here on the twentieth of the first month," said Marek Vale.

"Then we do not have to have that conversation, and I am not going to have it with you on a bank, and I have thought about how to do it twice this month and both times it was going to be about whether a man can be found, and it is not about that any more and it has not been for a fortnight.

"What it is about now is that I have a marshal's instrument and no way to put a name in it, and I have said the list of responsible parties out loud on a bank in front of eleven men, and I have crossed three of them off, and I have got two left and neither of them is a person, and the third month is when the low ground goes under.

"I am going to be on this reach at the sixth bell on the nineteenth of every month for as long as there is a reach to be on. That is not a promise and it is not a threat and it is not in any file of mine, and I would like it said out loud in front of a witness that I said it."

"You said it," said Marek Vale, "and I am the only witness anybody has got, and I am not going to pretend that is worth anything to you, and it is not, and you should not use it."

"I was not going to use it," said Seren Calder. "I wanted to have said it in front of a person and not in front of a barrow."

---

And down on the low-ground road that night the four sledges went into a store and the store was shut and the key went back on a nail, and eleven men went home in the dark along a lane that had not been a lane in the first month, and at the top of the reach there was a foot of clay with a hole nine feet long in the face of it and a trestle standing beside it with eleven names on it in a fair hand that had never been asked anything, and the third month was coming and there was no rate coming with it, and there was nobody in the basin who could be named.
