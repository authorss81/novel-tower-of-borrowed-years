# Chapter 178 — The Four Who Are Willing

Zvonko Maras came up the ladder on the seventeenth of the third month of MR 416 and stood on the two steps at the bottom of it for about four minutes with his hands behind his back and did not come up, and the boy of about nine who carries messages in that part of the city came up once to ask him whether he wanted a cup of something and then went down again without an answer.

"Three months," he said, at the top of the ladder, to nobody. "I have had a stone in my boot for about four seconds of it and I want it out and I am not going to ask anybody to do it."

"You can come up and sit down," said Zlata Hruska, "and nobody in this room is going to ask you a question, and that is not a courtesy, it is the standing business of the room and it started in the first month and it has never been suspended."

"Room," said Zvonko Maras.

He said the one word for about a second and then he came up the ladder, which took him longer than it took either of the two women, and he stood on the inside of the rail for the first time in his life, and did not go near the table, and looked at the rail round three sides of it for a while.

"Thirty-six years I have been in and out of that bay and there is a roof over it," he said. "A wall is nine foot of brick at the end of a lane and a man can go round a wall. A room has a floor. I know where I am on a floor and so does everybody who comes in after me, and I have been a person that somebody read a name off a sheet onto another sheet about, and I would like to be a person that stood on a floor."

---

There were four of them in the room and the fourth chair on the fourth side was not occupied and had not been occupied since the first month, and nobody had put anything on it.

Jelka Sondor is at the coast. She went on the second of the first month and has not come back, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a person may be told that she was on a wall, and nobody in this lane knew that she was on it at all until a carter brought a folded sheet up a ladder in the middle of the second lamp in the second month, and the woman who gave it to him at the coast said not one word about it and did not want a receipt and has not been asked anything since.

"She will have to be the fourth," said Zlata Hruska, "because a finding about four people and three bodies in it is a true copy of a mistake, and I have read that sentence off a book in a room on the causeway and I am not going to have it on a door."

So Topic read the four names out of his own head, which he had been given by a man with no office on a market road and which he had written on the slate with a head on it in the second month and had never written anywhere else, and then he put the slate down the other way up and said that he was not going to write the four down again anywhere and would somebody like to explain why not.

"Because you are going to be asked," said Zlata Hruska. "A list of what four people said in a room is a document, and a document is a thing a man in a good coat produces at somebody in about four years, and I have been at a door for nine years asking people one question and I have never written one of them down, and that has cost me every advantage I have and I am not going to lose it in a fortnight because somebody asked nicely."

---

"You want to know which four and how I know," said Zvonko Maras. "I have been up that ladder once before and I heard the woman on the second step say in a lane that the only way anybody in this basin finds out whether a person will stand in a room is to find out, and that is all of it, and it is not a discovery and it is not hers, and I am not going to sit here and be impressed by four people finding out the same thing on the same evening.

"I am going to say why I will stand in this room, and then the rest of you are going to say yours, and then we are going to find out what it is worth, and I am telling you now that I have worked it out on the ladder and it is nothing, and I have come up anyway, and there is a difference between those two facts and I would like somebody in this room to notice it."

"My ground came up in the ninth month of last year behind my wall and I did not ask for it and I got it because a man asked me first," said Odile Marn. "And I have been told nine times since the sixth of the month last year that I am a witness and not one of the nine has asked me a single question, and I have known since the sixth of the month last year that the reason nobody asks is a queue, and there is no box in a queue.

"So I am willing because a room is the only place in this basin where the asking is the whole of the business. That is all of it. I do not want a form and I do not want a rate and I do not want a ditch twice. I have got a ditch. I want to be in a room where somebody asks me what I thought I was doing, and then does not write it down."

---

"You have been produced," said Zlata Hruska, to Zvonko Maras.

"Once. In the sixth month of last year. In a queue with about nine people in it and a man in a good coat who said five words and asked me nothing at all." He did not sit down. "I was in a coal bay. I did not know until October and I have not been able to think about anything else since, and here is the thing I want in the air in this room, because I have got nobody to say it to and there is a whole district in this city of people who have been told things at a window and there is no room anywhere that any of them is in.

"I signed about that roof in the second month of a year six years ago because there was a boy in the household, and the boy is a man of about twenty-nine now and he pulls a truck out of the bay at the first bell, and I have never told him what I signed and I am not going to tell him in a room with four people in it.

"I will stand in a room. I will not have my name on a wall and I said that in a lane in the first month and I am not changing it. And the reason I will stand in a room is that somebody has my name and has had it since the sixth month of last year and it is in a book in a building, and I have been standing under it for six years without knowing.

"So I would like somebody to read it out. That is what I am willing for. I have never in my life wanted a thing read out and I have been in this basin thirty-six years and I am telling you the whole of it in one go because I am fifty-eight and I am not going to be thanked for it and I would rather be asked for something than be left on a step."

---

Topic had the slate face down on the table and his hand on it.

"I am the only one of the four whose paper is not a paper," he said. "Three of you have got sheets. I have got a copy of a page out of a drawer up that river, and a man with no office took it out of the drawer on the eleventh of the first month because a person of record may have a copy of a page and there is a form for that one, and it is the only one of the four that anybody in this kingdom has ever filled in for anybody else.

"I have read it at a rail. I read it on the ninth of the first month and I put the number up and I went back to work. I have counted at that rail for eleven years and I have counted about nine hundred people a morning and I have never once had a number put in front of me that I did not put there myself, and I am not going to start now, and I did not start then.

"So I am willing because I would like to be in a room where somebody reads out the number and it is the same number twice, and because I have never once in eleven years written anything down about a person, and I have been asked to today by a woman with a form at a counter, and I said no, and I am saying no again now, and I would like that on the same slate as everything else and I would like the same slate to go down the ladder afterwards."

"It goes down the ladder afterwards," said Zlata Hruska.

---

And then it went quiet for a while, because the fourth chair was still not occupied, and the lamp wanted trimming, and nobody trimmed it.

"The fourth is a woman of twenty-six at a coast," said Zlata Hruska, "and I am not going to guess her, and I am not going to have this room guess her, and I am going to say the reason and the reason is nine years old and it is the reason I do not write things down.

"If you do not know and you write something, you have made her. You have made her a person who said something in a room she has not been in, and there is no form anywhere in this kingdom on which she can be asked whether that is what she said, and in about four years it will be a thing a man can produce. So the fourth of these four is a gap, and the gap is a person, and the gap is the correct size and it is not going to be filled in from a chair.

"And the gap is not a thing you can get round to, either. The coast is four hours on a good day, and a boat's hire is a lease, and a lease is witnessed, and there is not one person in this lane who can be put in a box, and a man of forty-nine came up this ladder in the second month and told us that he has a boat and a piece of chalk and that a charter is not a lease and a lease is not a chalk, and that he could go and would not, and that he would not say so in a form, and none of us told him to and none of us has thought about it since, which is the worst thing any of us has done this month."

Nobody said anything about that, and the lamp went on wanting trimming.

---

"So here is the whole of it," said Zlata Hruska, "and I am going to say it because somebody has to, and I am not going to be thanked for it, and none of these three people are going to be thanked for it, and I have been in this lane nine years and I have never once seen anybody thanked and I do not think it is an oversight.

"Four people are willing. All four were produced once. A spent witness comes back when the paper is set aside, and the papers are not going to be set aside, and so the willingness is worth nothing and the arithmetic is correct and it is not a hardship.

"Being willing and being used are two different things, and the second one is the one that is on forms, and there is no form anywhere in this kingdom on which anybody may be entered as being willing. So these four people can be asked and cannot be stood up and cannot be produced and cannot be used, and if you take them you have not got a witness, you have got four people who came up a ladder."

Odile Marn looked at the wall.

"Then I am the ditch again," she said. "That is what I said at a counter in the second month and it has turned out to be the whole of the year. I asked for a ditch and I got a fortnight, and then I got a ditch, and I have got a woman of sixty-two on the other side of it who does not know she is going to be sent a season, and this room has spent three of the four people in it and has not touched the bill.

"Write nothing down," she said. "And do not ask me again. And if anybody in this lane ever tells you I have not been told, I have been told, and it was the sixth of the month last year in a rail queue, and I have been waiting about nine months for somebody to write that down and I have just watched you not write it down, and that is better, and I am not going to be grateful about it, because a person who is grateful in this lane gets a form afterwards."

---

Drazen Topic turned the slate over and read the four things out loud twice at the ordinary speed, and then he put the slate face down on the table again, and it was four numbers and no head and the numbers were not the number of people in the room and were not a figure about anybody, and they were thirty-six, four hundred, eleven, and a fourth that he said twice and did not say a third time because there was no fourth, and he read the gap the way he read everything else, at the ordinary speed, and the gap came out in the same voice as the other three.

That is what the room had, and it was nothing, and the paper on the door-shutter said in pencil that the room has no power to do anything whatever with any of it and is not a court and will not be called one, and the door-shutter was nine days off being painted and there was no form in this kingdom on which anybody may be asked to paint over a finding or not to.

And four people went down a ladder into a lane with no name, and a man of fifty-eight went down it backwards the way he had gone up it, and a chair stood empty at the fourth side of a rough square, and a woman of twenty-six was four hours away at a coast that could not be reached without a piece of paper, and nobody was thanked, and the four numbers stayed in four heads, which is the only kind of record in this basin that a man in a good coat cannot produce at anybody in about four years.
