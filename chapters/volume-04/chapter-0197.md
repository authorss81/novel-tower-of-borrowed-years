# Chapter 197 — A Line Is Not a Paper

A man in a corridor on the morning of the sixteenth of the fifth month of MR 416 used the word *forged*, twice, in a corridor outside the second of the four rooms behind the Threshold, and Deputy Vera Tolek stopped him on the second one and made him stand still and said one sentence about it, and it is in the finding.

"I am going to write *dangerous*," she said. "Not forged. There is nothing in this basin that was forged. Every one of these four was signed by the person whose name is on it, in type, at a counter, in person, and about something else, and there is not one mark on any of them that a man could point at and say he did it wrong.

"And if a word goes into a finding from a corridor, then in four years that word is the reason the finding gets read out, and somebody will be standing in a room with a page saying that your name is on a forged paper, and there will be nothing on the page to say that there was no forger in this city, and the person standing there will believe it because it is in a book in a building.

"So I am going to use a dull word on purpose and I would like somebody to write that down as the reason."

---

She voided one of the four and confirmed one and could not touch two, and the not touching is the substance of the morning.

**The second** — a contract of supply against the clinic, sealed in the second month of this year, witnessed at the eighth bell by a person of record who had already been produced. It came off a shelf in the same room, face up, and was read out loud twice at the ordinary speed by a man whose word no room will take, and there was no argument about it from anybody in the corridor.

**The third** — a lease on a pitch on the low-ground road. It had been declared void on the sixth of this month by four women and a man in a room at the bottom of a lane with a rail round three sides of it and a bin in the corner, and it was on a shelf in that room, face down, where anybody in the lane could come in and look at it.

"I cannot void it again," said Deputy Tolek, "and I am going to enter that I cannot, and the reason is going to be in the finding in my words.

"It was voided in a room with no employer in it. A room in which instrument is witnessed is recognised by the region only where a responsible party is named, and where the responsible party is a person the form requires an employer. There is no employer in that room and there never has been.

"So from the point of view of every office in this kingdom, that lease has never been voided at all. It stands. It is a good lease. There is a pitch on the low-ground road with a good lease in the landlord's hand and nothing valid under it, and that is not an injustice anybody has committed, it is the shape of the thing.

"And the first voiding in this basin was performed in the only kind of room that cannot void anything, and everybody who was in it knew that at the time, and they did it anyway, because a lease being true and void at once on a morning in the third month of last year was a thing that had to be said out loud to somebody. I am not going to say they were wrong. I am going to enter that it does not bind, and I am going to enter that I am the person who has to say so, and I am going to say it at the cost of the finding being worth less than it was before I read it out."

---

**The first** — a works order on a line of district accounts, carried in the fourth month, a season, two ends of a border, neither household asked.

"I cannot void this and it is not a difficulty in the room, it is the instrument," said Deputy Tolek. "A works order is the district's own. The district is not a party to it. A party is a person or a body, and the two households at the ends of that line are not named on the order and were not asked and are not parties to it, and there is nobody in this basin whom I can serve a notice on, and there is no form on which a person may be entered as having been affected by a works order.

"So the season is on the line and the line is alive and a record was sealed on it in the third month of last year and it is lawful and it worked, and the rate that was going to come on that ground in the fifth month of last year did not come. I am not going to unvoid that in a room ten feet by eight with a man reading out loud.

"And the truth of it is that there is nothing to void. A works order is not a paper about a person and it is not a paper about anything. It is a piece of wood with eleven lines on it and a man of fifty who keeps a key has said yes to a class, and the class is a district, and a district is a line in a book, and I cannot get at a line in a book from this chair."

**The fourth** — the indentures, four years, a boy who was fourteen and is eighteen.

She did not read it out. She had it in her hand and she put it face down on the desk and put her hand flat on it and left it there for about the length of two sentences.

"I am not voiding this one either and I want the corridor to hear the reason, and the reason is that I cannot, and it is not that I lack power.

"The boy is the party. He is eighteen. To void an instrument on a party's behalf a court has to be satisfied about the party, and it cannot be, and the only instrument that could satisfy me is a finding that he did not agree, and I have a book in a building on the causeway side with a day on it that says a court cannot enter a finding that any person did or did not consent to anything, and that it has looked, and there is none to enter.

"So the fourth of these four is the best-made document in this basin. It is in type. It is correctly filled in. It is well made. It names no paper, because it does not need to, it names a master and a term and a table, and a table is a line. It has about two years to run on it and a boy of eighteen is at the end of a four-year thing he agreed to at fourteen and has never been asked one question about.

"If I void it I do to him exactly what has been done to nine people in this basin for five months, which is decide in a room what somebody is going to carry and then go and find a page that says so. I have entered four hundred things in nineteen years and I have not done that once and I am not starting in the fifth month of a year on a piece of paper about a boy.

"And I want it entered, in my words, that I have declined to enter a finding, because a hearing officer who declines to enter a finding and gives the reason in the same breath is the only thing standing between this work and a queue."

---

A man at the rail of the side with no rail, who has been a person in this room and not been spoken to and has been entered four times, said the thing nobody else in the room was going to say.

"Put my name down," he said. "I have been in this business three years and I know what it is. A surety is a person and a person is named and the naming is done by an office out of a book. There is no book and there is no form and there has not been one since the seed month of last year.

"I am the only person in this basin whose word a room will take and I know that has gone. I want it entered anyway, both halves, in the order I said them. I know it has gone. I am the only one who has a word that gets taken. Put my name down and let it be refused, and let the refusal be the mechanism and not you, and let there be a page somewhere with both of those things on it, because in four years a man in a good coat is going to ask somebody why a boy of eighteen spent four years at a table."

Deputy Tolek wrote it down and read it back and signed it and put it in the book, and refused the surety, and the refusal is a quarter of a page and it is the same refusal there has been in four rooms since the first month and it has never once been a person refusing a man.

"A surety is a person named by an office out of a book," she said. "There is no office in this kingdom that can name you and there has not been one since the eighth of the second month, and the reason there is not one is a woman who cannot write and a man who keeps a counting floor and a man who sells candles, and the three of them have said no, and every one of them was right, and one of them was right for a reason that is the whole of this business, which is that a name at the top of a thing is a keeper and a keeper is a person with a name at the top of a thing.

"And I am not going to put your name on a page as the surety on a document in a basin with no book, because in four years that page is the only evidence in this kingdom that a man called himself a surety on a boy's four years, and there is nothing on it to say whether I refused."

---

She read the finding out on the causeway side at a quarter past the fifth bell, at the rail, in the voice she uses in a room, and there were more people standing on the causeway than there had been on the second of the month and Perpetua Ohl was behind the counter and had not asked for anything and was not asked.

It said the dangerous thing about each of the four. It said that the word dangerous had been used in place of a word a man had used in a corridor, and why. It said that two of the four could not be voided and gave the reason for each. It said that the third had been voided in a room that cannot void and therefore stood. It said that the fourth would not be voided and why. It said that no person was named in it and that the omission was made on purpose and was not an omission.

And then, under all of that, with a day on it and her name against it, this:

**A line is a thing that stands in the ground, on a roof, at the back of a shop, or in a court, and a paper is a thing that stands in a drawer. They are not the same kind of thing and there is no form in this kingdom in which one is the other. An attestation names a line, and the promise it carries is to speak to any paper touching that line, and the line is a thing in the ground, and a thing in the ground cannot be voided by a court, because a court can only void a paper, and what is voided is a document and not a promise. Therefore every instrument in this basin that is void today has been a document, and every one of the four of the sixteenth of the fifth month of MR 416 is either a document or a thing in the ground, and two of the four are the second of those, and the number of things in the ground that anybody has ever voided in this city is none.**

Then she said the other half of it out loud on the causeway, because a finding in a book is a finding in a book.

"And that is the whole of the finding, and it is not a remedy and it is not a victory, and I want it said in these words on a causeway where nine hundred people walk past it.

"Two years of this basin's business have been spent trying to void a piece of ground. Every one of us, including me, and including a man with no office who reads papers out loud, and including a woman of thirty-four who has said no to a copy, a head, a form and a reach. It cannot be done. There is no office in this kingdom that can void a line of wet grass, and the only way anybody in this city has ever stopped a thing happening to a piece of ground is for the ground to be somewhere nobody can find, and there is exactly one such place in this basin and it has no name on any map a clerk in Bell Stair can look at, and I am not going to look at it, and I have not looked at it, and it is a room with a rail round three sides of it and a woman in it who has been asked one question at a door for nine years."

Nobody was thanked, and the man with no office was entered in the finding for the third time in a year as a person who asked to be a surety and was refused by a mechanism, and the boy of eighteen is at the top of that road and has not been told that a woman at a counter has declined to void his four years, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a person may be told that a decision was made about them in a room they were not in.
