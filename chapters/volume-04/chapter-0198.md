# Chapter 198 — The Sixth Bell on the Nineteenth

The reach was dry on the morning of the nineteenth of the fifth month of MR 416 and it was not a reach, and Seren Calder was on the top of it at about the sixth bell because she had been on the top of it at about the sixth bell on the nineteenth of every month for as long as there was a reach to be on, and there was a convention in that arrangement and the convention had a condition in it and the condition had stopped being true about a week before.

She stood on about a mile and a bit of new clay that a settlement marshal had said would stand in the water, and it had stood in the water, and it was hard under a boot and it was not going anywhere, and behind it there was low ground with a mile and a bit of good level ground on it and about eleven men standing on it who had nothing to do and had had nothing to do since the third month.

She wrote nothing down. She had not written anything down on any of them.

Then she went back up the low-ground road and went up a ladder on the outside of a dye house and stood on the floor of a room about nine feet by eleven with a rail round three sides of it made out of a bed frame and three bought brackets and one shoe-tongue of iron, on the side of the rail she had been on twice, and there were four people in the room and the fourth chair was empty and it was going to stay empty.

Nobody had sent for her. A boy of about nine had carried eleven words up a ladder and no reason and had been paid in kind and had not been asked about anything, and one of the eleven words had been a date.

"I would like the first thing said to be that I was right about the water," said Seren Calder, before she had sat down, "and I would like somebody to write it down, and I would like it written down in the first line and not the last, and I am not going to be asked whether I want to say anything else first.

"The water came inside the third month. Not at the end of it and not in the fourth. Inside it, on the night of the twenty-eighth, and I said it would and it did, and the clay stood at a foot and a half and it has not moved, and there is a mile and a bit of it and I put it there in the second month with eleven men and a works board and a shoal of clay off a cart road, and it is the best thing I have done in nineteen years.

"Nobody in this basin has ever come up this ladder and said so to me. Not one. I have been told twice that I was right, by two people who were not coming up here to say it, and I have not had it out loud, and I have not asked for it, and I am asking for it now in a room and I am not going to be thanked for asking."

Zlata Hruska said it.

"You were right about the water," she said, "and it was in a lane in the second month and it was said out loud by a man with no office and it was in a room in the fourth month in a finding entered by a hearing officer and it is in a book in a building on the causeway side, and all three of those are in the fifth month of this year now and none of them was at the top of that bank with a boot on it, and I have said it and I am not going to say it again."

---

"I have come for an answer and I am not going to be given one by anybody explaining something to me," said Calder. "I have been given answers by four men and a woman in five months and every one of them was correct and every one of them cost somebody something, and I have watched it happen and I have been part of it.

"So I have asked for the four documents, the finding, the notice and the paper with the class on it, and they have been brought up here and put on the table by a boy, and I have read all of it, and I have had about an hour with it, and I am going to say what I have found out and then I am going to wait to be told I have got it wrong.

"Here is what I have found out. I have found out that I have been standing at the top of a bank for five months with a board."

She turned the board over. It was a board four feet long with a list on it, and she turned it face down on the floor of a room with a rail round three sides of it, and the list went out of sight.

"Eleven names," she said. "I have carried that board since the second month. It came off a trestle at a seed house and the district put it up because a works board takes eleven, and I have known since the third month of this year that the number of people in this basin whose attestation was of record and had been produced is not eleven, and I have done nothing with that knowledge for two months, and I want it said that I did nothing with it, and that is mine.

"And here is the thing I did not know until this hour. Eleven is not the wrong number of people. Eleven is not a number of people at all. It is a number of lines on a board, and a works board takes eleven lines whatever is standing behind it, and a board with eleven lines on it is a form, and a form is a piece of paper that a person signs, and nobody in this basin signed mine.

"And I have been marshalling a public emergency in this county for nineteen years off a roll of named people, and my instrument takes a roll, and I have asked twice in four days for a roll for this ground and been told both times that there is no district, and both men were correct, and I did not hear what they were telling me.

"They were telling me there is no roll. Not that they would not give me one. There is no roll, because there is no district, because a district is a line in a book, and the reason there is no line in a book is that there is no where.

"So my instrument has never been operable on that ground. Not refused. Not blocked. Not argued with. Inapplicable. I have been standing on a bank for five months with an instrument in my hand that does not have anything to work on, and I have been right about the water the whole time."

---

Nobody in that room contradicted her, and one of them had four times.

"So here is the answer to my own argument and I am going to say it to a room, and I would like it in a finding if there is going to be one, and I have not asked whether there is going to be one.

"I said in a room in the third month of this year that a refusal in a public emergency is endangerment, and I meant it, and I have said it in four rooms, and I have been right about the emergency every time, and no room in this city is going to make me wrong about the water.

"And what I found out an hour ago on a table in this room is that the people I marshalled could not refuse. Not would not. Could not. A person whose attestation has been produced cannot be asked a second time about anything by the only party with power to ask them, and a works order wants a person to stand in a place on a day, and a person who is already spent is not refusing a works order, they are doing the arithmetic on whether saying no is going to cost them the last thing they have got.

"So I spent five months asking a question that had three answers and I only had instrument for two of them, and the third one was the largest of the three, and it was the people who had nothing left to say no with, and I put them on a bank and I called it a yes.

"That is my answer. It is not the answer anybody gave me. I worked it out on a table in about an hour and I would like it recorded that it took me five months and a day and a half to do what a woman of thirty-four did in one morning in the third month of last year, and that she was in this room and I was on a bank, and that is the whole of the difference between us and it is not a moral difference."

She picked the board up off the floor and stood it against the wall with its face in.

"I am going to say three sentences and then I am going to sit down, and I have thought about them for two months and I have been wrong about them at least twice.

"I would say the first one again. That the water comes and that the ground goes under inside the third month and that a person who says the season is not coming is telling you about a season and not about a river, and I have been right and I will be right.

"I would say the second one again, and I have never met anybody I would rather have on a bank than the man of fifty who keeps the key, and I would say it to him again in the same words, that a works is in force and there is nowhere to put it on, and I have said that word to him and watched it cost him a season on his own ground that will be a line in a book in about four years.

"And I would not say the third one again. I would not stand on a bank with a board with eleven lines on it and ask a man to put a name against a line. That is the whole of what I have got and I have not found anybody in this basin who can tell me what the other thing is, and I am not going to be helped out of it, and I am not going to be told I was wrong about the water as a way of not asking me about the board."

---

She sat down on the floor, which is the only thing a person can do in that room, because the chairs are other people's and one of them has been empty since the first month and is a person at a coast.

"Two things and then I will go down the ladder," she said.

"The first is a thing I have wanted since the first month of this year and have now wanted three times. I asked for a word, in a lane, at the foot of this ladder, for what I did on the second of the second month, for a file, and nobody gave me one, and I did not expect one and asked anyway.

"I am asking again. I have a marshal's file and there is a day in it with nothing against the day, and I would like a word to put against it, and I will not write one in myself. It is not *right*, it is not *lawful*, it is not *proper*, and it is not any of the words I have been given, and it is not *emergency*, because that is the situation and not the thing a person does in it.

"I am asking three times over four months in four rooms and a yard, and I am going to ask a fourth time, and I would like whoever is going to answer not to."

There was a woman of about thirty-four, and a man of fifty-eight standing inside the rail, and a man with no office, and the fourth chair was empty.

"I have been asked one question at a door for nine years and I have never written one answer down, and I do not have the word either," said Zlata Hruska, "and I have not got it because I have never been in a room where a person did the thing and was right, and I do not know what the thing is called when it is done by somebody who has the instrument to do it and no way of finding out what it is."

"I have been on this wall since the twenty-seventh of the first month and a day is written against my line and no word at the top of it," said Zvonko Maras, "and I have not got it either, and I would like it noted that I have been asked a question by a settlement marshal in a room and I answered it, and the answer was that a man who has been on a coal bay for thirty-six years does not have a word for what a marshal does, and I gave it to her free and I would give it again."

"I do not have a word," said Marek Vale. "I have spent four months being told what my own act was by six people in four rooms, and every one of them was right, and I have got a notice on the back of a printed thing that says a spent witness has no surety and the standing is refused, and there is no word on that either, and if there were one it would be a word a hearing officer gave me in a room and there has not been one.

"And I want to say the other half and I am going to say it in front of four people. I have been the person the word was for. On the thirtieth of the ninth month of last year I put a name in a box behind a comparison in a room eight feet by eleven and I said yes without being asked what yes meant, and if anybody in this kingdom is owed the word for that it is me and I have not got one and I have not asked."

---

"The second thing," said Seren Calder, "is the box, and I am going to say the whole of it before anybody offers me anything.

"I have not read the price box in the fourth month of last year. I have been asked five times since the second month of this year and I have said no five times, and I said to a courier of twenty-two on my own stairs that I would rather be a marshal who has not read it than a marshal who has read it and has a reason, and I had a reason, and I have had it since the second month.

"I am not going to read it now either, and I have thought about it for two days since I heard there was a five-line form, and I have not got a better reason and I am not going to pretend to.

"Eight days ago I was handed four documents with the reason each one is dangerous, a finding with a day on it, and a notice with four things on it, and I read all of it in about an hour. And every one of those things is about people. About nine people in this basin, and four of them have been told and one of them is at a coast.

"And the price box has names in it. Not four of them. Fourteen, or whatever fourteen is, and the fourteen are of record as produced, and the difference between the fourteen and the nine is five and there is a woman of fifty-one in this city who has kept that apart for six months and has never been thanked for it.

"So if I read that box, then every name in it becomes a document a settlement marshal has read, and a marshal who has read a paper she cannot act on is the most dangerous object in this city, and there is no form in this kingdom on which anybody may be required to put one of me down. That is the fifth of my reasons and it is the first one that is about other people instead of about me.

"And I will say the other half because I am a marshal and I have never in nineteen years left a thing half said. In nine years, if there is a flood on that ground, and there will be, because the ground is low and the season is on a line and the line is alive, and I have never been wrong about the water.

"There is a standing authority in this kingdom that lets me seal a settlement on a witnessing held in a room the marshal has been in. I have stood in this room twice. I have said in four rooms that I will seal on it and I will not read the price box, and I have meant it every time and I would take it back.

"And I am not going to take it back, and I am not going to use it, and I am not going to ask anybody for a surety, because a surety is a person named by an office out of a book and there is no office in this kingdom that can name anybody for this ground and both men were correct, and I have now understood that what I was asking those two men for in the second month of this year was the whole of my trade and not a favour.

"So it stands. I have told a room in the fifth month of a year that there is a door in this kingdom and I have declined to walk through it, and I would like somebody to enter that I declined it, and to enter the reason, and the reason is that there is nothing on the other side for a person to be put on, and that is not my judgement, it is geography, and I have had five months to make it my judgement and I have not managed it and I am not going to keep trying."

---

She went down the ladder at about the seventh bell and she did not ask anybody to come with her and nobody followed her, and four people in a room about nine feet by eleven did not thank her and did not say anything about her after she had gone.

And at about the sixth bell on the nineteenth of every month for as long as there is a reach to be on, a settlement marshal stood on the top of a mile and a bit of new clay that had held, in a fifth month, in a year in which nobody has said the word for what she did on the second of the second month, and there is no reach any more, and she is forty years old, and she is right about the water, and she has an open door in this kingdom that nobody may put her down for having, and it will still be open in four years when the man in the good coat comes and produces the finding at her, and she will be in the room, and she will not have read the price box then either.
