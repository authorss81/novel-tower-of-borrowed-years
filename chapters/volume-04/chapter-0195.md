# Chapter 195 — Good Against One Thing

Two forms were in use in this basin by the twelfth of the fifth month of MR 416 and a man in a wet sleeve put them side by side on a table over a counting floor in Rainmarket with a stone on each so that the wind could not have them, and about eleven people stood round, and he did not explain them. He waited to be asked.

They are the same form. Four ruled lines and no boxes, the name, the line, a printed third line in type, and a date that is a month and not a day. One of them has four ruled lines and one of them has five. That is the whole difference and it is a quarter of an inch of type that a woman took out of a forme nineteen years ago to save a quarter of a sheet on about four hundred copies, and it was cut back into a forme in a copy room in Bell Stair on the fourth of this month at ten minutes to the sixth bell, and there is a day on the wrapper and a price chalked on it in the same clerk's hand.

"Ask," he said.

---

A man who keeps a counting floor for nine years asked, and he asked well, because he has wanted to be told no quickly since the second of the fourth month and has not had a chance of asking a question that he expected a yes to.

"Read me the fifth one," he said. "There is nothing on the fifth one. I have looked at it from here and there are two words and there is a space and there is nothing in the space."

"There is nothing in the space," said Marek Vale. "That is the finding and it took nine seconds in a copy room and two days in three rooms to get to and I am going to say it as a fact and not as a thing I have thought of.

"The two words say the paper. That is all they do. They are a head on a blank, and a blank with a head on it is the only instruction on the whole sheet, and it has been off the form for nineteen years, and it went back on four days ago, and in four days nobody in this basin has been able to fill it in.

"The reason is on the third line. I will speak to any paper touching this line while I live. That is a promise about a line and it is the oldest thing in this business. The fifth line is a promise about a paper instead, and a paper is written after the form is signed, because the form is what the sealing is done on top of. So the sheet asks a person at a counter to name a piece of paper that is not a piece of paper yet."

"Then what good is it," said Danko Pehlin.

"It is good," said Marek Vale, "about nine seconds after the paper exists, to a person who is in the room with it, and there is exactly one such person in this basin for every instrument, and she is a hearing officer, and she is the only person in this city whose word a room will take."

---

"You have got a paper on that table," said Deputy Vera Tolek, from the rail, where she had been standing for about a minute and a half without saying anything, "that is good against one thing, and you have not looked at what else it is standing up.

"The old one. Four lines. A line of wet grass between two seed yards, the growth month of MR 416 five years back, and a name, and a month, and a printed third line.

"Every paper that has ever touched that line since that month is standing on that sheet. Not one paper. All of them. A works board. A rate. A fortnight on the ground and a trench that was not dug. Whatever has been laid on that line in five years, it is standing on the shoulder of a woman of thirty-one who signed about a ditch because a man from the water office said it would be doing the district a favour and because she wanted the wall mended, and she was free when she signed it, and she has never been asked one question about any of the other things.

"That is the harm and it is nineteen years old and it is the best-made piece of paper in this basin.

"And the new one says a paper. And if a new one says a paper, then an old one, by comparison, says every paper. And I want to say that carefully because it is the most frightening sentence I have said in a room in nineteen years and I have not said it in a room before.

"Restoring a line does not stop the old sheets doing what they have been doing. It makes what they have been doing visible. From today, a four-line sheet is not a sheet about a thing that happened to be loose. It is a sheet about a line, and a line speaks to every paper that touches it, and there is a great many papers touching a great many lines in this basin, and every one of them is standing on somebody."

The counting floor went quiet for about the length of a press-pull.

"Then put them all aside," said Danko Pehlin. "All the four-line ones. Get them in and set them aside, and then we are all right."

"There is no form in this kingdom on which a paper may be set aside," said Deputy Tolek, "and that is not the reason and I am not going to let it be the reason. The reason is that setting a paper aside does not stop a line from touching paper, it only stops one document, and there are about nine hundred four-line forms in drawers in offices in this city from the twenty-first of the fourth month alone and they went out on carts and on backs, and every one of them is well made and correctly filled in and not one of them has a defect in it.

"And I am not going to say there is no form, because a woman of thirty-four said no to a form in this business in the third month of last year and she was right, and I have said no to a great many things and I am not going to pretend that this is a form problem. This is a paper problem and paper problems go on for as long as there is paper."

---

The lease on the back room behind the chandler came up because a woman of twenty-two brought it up, and she brought it because she was in the lane and the chandler was in his street door and had asked her, in the ordinary way, a question about a bin.

"There is a lease on this ground," said Nim Rook, "and I am not going to say whose, and I have not come up this lane about it, and I have told you the reason I have told you anything at all, which is that a woman read out a form in a room in the second month of this year and the name on the bottom of it is a woman of about twenty-six who went to a coast in the first month of this year and has not come back, and whose line on that form is a bin behind a chandler's, and the word a is doing a good deal of work in it and I have been at the end of this lane twice since and I have not gone round to the other end of it.

"So I am going to say the thing and then I am going to go down the lane.

"Everything standing on that lease is standing on a bin. It has been standing on a bin since the growth month of MR 412, and it is a good bin and a good form and the woman signed it about a bin and was free when she signed it, and the bin is a line like any other line and a line speaks to every paper that touches it, and a lease on a back room is a paper and it touches the bin because the bin is standing in the corner of the back room and has done since four women carried it up this lane on the backs of four women on the twenty-second of the second month of this year.

"And nobody can tell you that. That is the sentence I have come up the lane to say and I have said it and I am going.

"Not because it is cruel. Because you have asked twice in four years who owns that bin and have been told nothing both times, and you have decided to go on not knowing, and you have told this lane that you have decided it and that it will cost you something at some point. And it has. It has cost you a back room in the fifth month of a year, and you did not have to be told a single word of it, and nobody in this lane is going to tell you, and I am going down the stairs and I am not going to come up this lane about a bin again."

The chandler stood in his street door and did not say anything for about a minute.

"Two years," he said. "I have not swept it and I have not thrown it out and I have not asked, and it has taken two years and four minutes, and I would like it noticed that I have not asked one question about a bin this morning and that a woman of twenty-two has come up my lane and told me the whole of it anyway, and I would like it noticed by somebody, and there is nobody here I am going to say it to, and I have been in this lane nine years and there are nine of you and not one of you is going to remember this in the sixth month."

---

Nobody did remember it in the sixth month. That is what happened next and it is the reason for everything else in this chapter, and it was not anybody's doing.

Deputy Tolek signed one instrument. One, herself, on a five-line form, at the counter on the causeway side at about the second bell on the twelfth of the fifth month, and she named the paper in the fifth line, and it was the first time in nineteen years that anybody in this kingdom had done that, and the paper was a cart-hire for a fortnight on the low-ground road, and it was the smallest instrument she could find.

She gave the cost out loud before the pen was near it, in the room, in front of about fourteen people, and the cost is hers and it is not the same cost as anybody else's.

"I am a hearing officer of the Chamber and I have been nineteen years in it," she said. "I have entered four hundred things. I have never been a person of record. There is no attestation in this kingdom with my name on it and I have never been produced and I have never been at a window and told five words.

"And I am about to be. In one minute I am going to put my name in the fourth line of a sheet, on a form, at a counter, on a day, and a counter in this kingdom is allowed to write a day, and then I am a person whose attestation is of record, and the only way anybody in this basin will ever find out that I am a witness to anything is to produce me once, and I can never be produced twice, and after that I am the thing I have spent nineteen years taking people apart from.

"And I am doing it because the fifth line works. I have put the name of a paper in the fifth line of a form and it fills, and the paper is a cart-hire and a fortnight and two carts and a man who has nobody to ask, and it is the only thing in this basin the fifth line can be used for today and I am going to use it on it.

"And I am not going to let anybody in this room tell me not to, because I have read every finding I have entered in two years and not one of them has ever cost the person it was about anything, and I have wanted one of them to cost somebody something since the fourth of the first month, and this is me, and I chose it, and I would like it entered that I said the cost before the pen and not after."

She put her name in the fourth line. She read the sheet out loud twice at the ordinary speed before she did it, which is what a clerk does and which is the only qualification Marek Vale has and which he does not have, and she named the cart-hire in the fifth line, and the counter wrote the day, and the man got his two carts for a fortnight.

And Deputy Vera Tolek, forty-four, nineteen years a hearing officer of the Chamber, is now a person of record in this basin, and has been produced once, and cannot be produced again, and is not thanked for it and did not want to be, and has not been told that she was brave, and would not have thanked anybody for it.

---

And a man with no office stood in a lane at about the third bell on the twelfth of the fifth month of MR 416 with a coat on and a reason, and did not go.

The coast is four hours away. There is a road to it. There is a charter and the charter needs a witness, and the witness would be a woman of twenty-six who is one of the four that cannot be found and has not come back and was told on the twenty-sixth of the third month by a carter's mouth that she is a witness to a thing.

He could have gone. There was nothing to stop him. He had been up a river a night and a day and back twice since the seed month and he had walked into a chamber a night and a day up this river and been neither refused nor served, and there is no form in this kingdom on which anybody may be told not to go somewhere, and there never has been one.

"That is a road," said Nim Rook. "That is a four-hour road and a charter on it and a woman at the end of it, and if you set off now you would be back before the sixth bell and there is nobody in this basin who could tell you that you had not, and I have not asked you to do it and I am not going to.

"But I am going to say the sentence, because I am a courier and this is my trade and nobody else in this lane can say it properly. The door of my trade is a road. It is not in a book and it is not in a district and it does not have a name on any map a clerk in Bell Stair can look at, and neither does this lane, and I do not know what the two of them have got to do with each other and I have thought about it since the thirteenth of the fourth month and I have not got an answer.

"And if I ask you to go, then this lane has sent a man, and a room that sends a man has a reach, and a reach is an area, and an area is a district, and a district is a line in a book, and the day that happens this room is an office and the ladder is a door of an office. That is the fifth no and it was said on the twenty-first of the third month by a woman of about thirty-four and it is the one that keeps all four of the others standing up.

"So I am not asking you, and you were not going to, and I would like it noted that I put the argument in and you did not need it."

Nobody noted it. The woman of twenty-two put her hands in her coat and went down the lane, and the man with no office went up the hill, and there is a bin in the corner of a back room in a lane that has no name on it that nobody has swept and nobody has thrown out, and a woman at a coast has not been told, and a Deputy of the Chamber is a spent witness with a day on a counter's page, and nobody in this basin was thanked for anything.
