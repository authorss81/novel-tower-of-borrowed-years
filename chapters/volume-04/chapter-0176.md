# Chapter 176 — A Challenge Brought by the Marshal

A challenge is free, anybody may bring one, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a challenge may be refused, and on the tenth of the third month of MR 416 a settlement marshal brought one to the second of the four rooms behind the Threshold, which is ten feet by eight and is the room a challenge is heard in, and she was forty and she had been in this basin about two months and she was the officer of the Crown March who sealed the reach.

Perpetua Ohl entered it at the first bell and could not refuse it and did not try, and she brought it up herself and stood at the rail with her hands behind her back in the way of a person who has delivered nine hundred things at a counter and has never once stayed to watch.

The hearing officer was Deputy Vera Tolek, forty-four, who had been a hearing officer for nineteen years and had been two years in this room, and who had a book and three sheets of foolscap, and who looked at the person at the front of the rail for about four seconds and then said:

"State what you are challenging."

"I have nothing to challenge," said Seren Calder. "That is the whole of it and I have come a mile to say it."

---

"Then I cannot hear it," said Deputy Tolek.

"You cannot refuse it either."

"I cannot refuse it and I am not going to, and I want the distinction said out loud in this room before anything else happens, because it is the whole of what this room is and the people in the doorway are about to misunderstand it.

"A challenge is a request that a sheet be looked at before it is sealed. It is free. Anybody may bring one. There is no form on which it may be refused, and I have never refused one, and I was not going to start with a marshal.

"And a challenge requires a sheet. There is no sheet. You have told me so yourself, in the first four words out of your mouth, and that is a fact and not a complaint, and it puts the matter in a place this court has no instrument for at all, which is not *refused* and is not *heard*. It is before me and it is not a thing I can do."

"Then what are you going to do with me."

"I am going to let you stand there," said Deputy Tolek, "and I am going to tell you the three things I am allowed to do in this room, and then you are going to tell me which of the three you came for, and I am telling you in advance that I do not think you came for the third one either."

---

Nim Rook had not come and had sent nothing, and a boy of about nine who carried messages for anybody in that part of the city had carried a sentence up from a man at a counter and had not been asked what it was about, and the sentence was that if a marshal brings a challenge with nothing in it then the room is the room and the marshal is the marshal and neither of them has anything to be sorry for.

Deputy Tolek read the sheet and put it down.

"You have about nine people in the doorway," she said, "and there is a door on this room that is eleven inches open and the corridor behind it is not a room, and I am going to ask you once whether you want the door shut or open, and if you say open I am going to leave it open and I am going to hold every word of this in this room, and if you say shut I am going to shut it and you can stand in the corridor and be heard through eleven inches, which is what about two thirds of the business of this kingdom is done through."

"Open," said Seren Calder. "I have been in four rooms in a month with a table at the head of them and I have been the table at the head of one of them, and I would like this one to have about nine people in the doorway."

---

"Very well," said Deputy Tolek, "and I am going to say the three things now, and then you are going to say your argument, and I am going to write it down, and I am going to tell you before you start that the writing down is not neutral and it is not free and it is not nothing, and I will come back to that at the end.

"First. I can hear a sheet. There is no sheet.

"Second. I can enter a finding of fact, a finding of arithmetic, a finding of conduct, or a finding of record. I have been entering them for nineteen years and I have a hand that is worse than it was.

"Third. I can refuse a challenge. There is no form on which I may, I have never done it, and I am not going to do it to a settlement marshal in the third month."

"Then there is nothing you can do."

"There is one thing I can do," said Deputy Tolek, "and it is the reason I have not sent you away, and I am not going to pretend to you that I have worked out whether it is a good idea. I can hear what you say and write it down, and a writing down in this room goes into a book in a building on the causeway side and it stays there as long as the building stands, and it will have a day on it, because a court in a building has a day and there is no other date a court in a building can use.

"That is a document. I am about to make a document. I have thought about that for about a minute and a half and I am going to do it anyway, and I would like it noticed that a marshal asked for it and a Deputy heard it."

---

"Then here it is, and it is the second time I have said this and the first time I have said it to a room."

Seren Calder put her hands on the rail and did not touch anything else on the desk.

"The ground goes under in the third month. That is not a fear and it is not a figure anybody has made up. It is what the water does and I have been doing this nineteen years and I have never once known it to do otherwise, and the works on that reach stopped a month ago and the sledges are in a store and the clay has gone soft and there is nobody left on it who can be asked to stand in it.

"And I cannot seal a settlement. A settlement in a public emergency may be sealed on a surety, and a surety is a person, and a person is named, and the naming is done by an office out of a book.

"And here is the part I have been carrying about this basin for a month and have not been able to put down and have said out loud on a bank and on a reach and in a room over a dye house and have been answered in none of them.

"A rule that says a person's name may only be used if an office can produce it is not a neutral rule. It is a rule about who the city can reach.

"Because the people the city cannot name are the people on the lowest ground. Not the unlucky ones. The lowest. That is not a metaphor and it is not a sentence. A district account cannot be struck out and cannot be struck, and this ground is on a line of accounts that a loan kept alive in the third month of last year, and the people on this ground are the people who are on it, and the people on it are the people whose names are not in the books in this city, because the books in this city have never had this ground on them.

"So the city has three answers for a person, and the third answer is silence. And the record does not have a box for silence. It has a box for yes and a box for no and nothing at all for the third, and a thing that is not in a box is not a no.

"And that is the whole of it and I want it in your book in those words. In about four years a man in a good coat will produce a settlement for this reach and it will be witnessed by eleven names and every one of the eleven names will be a person the city can produce, and the finding will be that the district consented, and the district consented is going to be read off eleven names and the eleven names are going to be people from a district that has a roll, while the people who are actually on the ground are going to have said nothing at all, and their nothing is going to be the finding.

"That is not going to happen to me. It has already happened to about nine people in this basin, one at a time, at a window, and there is no form in this kingdom on which any of the nine was asked, and it took the nine five months and a queue."

---

Deputy Tolek wrote for about eleven minutes and did not interrupt once and did not ask a single question, and when she had finished she read it back out loud twice at the ordinary speed, which is what a person does in a room that has learned anything at all, and then she said:

"I am going to say four things and I am going to be exact, and the first of them is against you and the other three are against me, and you are going to want the fourth one most and I am not going to give it to you.

"First. I believe you, and it makes no difference whatever, and I want that entered as well, because a court that hears an argument it accepts and then says the argument makes no difference is at least honest about the shape of itself.

"Second. I have no power. A hearing officer of the Chamber can make a finding of fact, arithmetic, conduct or record. I cannot make a policy and I cannot change an instrument and I cannot tell a district office that a rule of nine years standing is a rule that cannot do what it is for, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a hearing officer may say that a rule is wrong, and if I said it in this room it would not be a finding. It would be a woman with a book saying a thing at eight bells.

"Third. And this is the one that matters. There is a finding of the fourth of the first month in a book in a building and it says that this court cannot enter a finding that any person did or did not consent to anything, and that the court has looked for one and there is not one. Your argument is an argument about consent. It is the best argument about consent I have heard in nineteen years and I am telling you that it cannot go in, and it is not going in because the box does not exist, and the box not existing is not a thing a court fixes."

---

"And the fourth," said Marek Vale, from the side of the room with no rail, where a man stands when he is not wanted to be looked at.

"The fourth is hers," said Deputy Tolek. "Go on."

"The finding of the fourth of last month says this court has no question it can put to a person about a paper other than a question about their name," said Marek Vale. "This is not a question about anybody's name. It is a question about a rule, and a rule is not a person, and the court can find a fact about a rule the same way it finds a fact about a form."

"That is true and it is nine seconds' work and it does not help her," said Deputy Tolek. "Mr Vale, I am going to say one thing about you and then I am not going to say it again this month.

"On the fourth of the first month you stood at the back of this room and I entered your statement, and I entered it because the words of a man standing in a room are enterable, whatever else they are. And I have thought about that for about two months and I have not changed my mind and I am not going to, and what I am going to say is that I did it because I had no alternative and not because I had a box for it.

"So I can find a fact about a rule. I cannot find a fact about a person being free. And you know that, and she knows that, and the people in that doorway do not, and the difference between those two things is the whole distance between this city and anything that would be worth doing."

---

Nobody said anything in the room for a while, and the door was eleven inches open and there were nine or ten people in it and the boy was on the step with a message he had not been asked about.

"The fourth thing," said Deputy Tolek. "I am not going to make a policy. I am going to make a finding of fact, of record, and of conduct, and it is going to be short, and it is going to have a day on it, and it is going to be the first document in this basin in about a year that says in type that an instrument cannot do what it is for.

"Whatever else is true, that is a fact, and it is one I can prove, and I am going to enter it.

"And I am going to tell you what it is going to cost, because you are a marshal and you have been exact with me and I am not going to have you telling anybody afterwards that you did not know.

"The moment that document is in a book, it is in a book. In about four years a man in a good coat can produce it at me, at you, at this doorway, at a clerk in a good coat, and it will say a true thing, and there will be nothing in this kingdom that can take it out of the book, and the taking of it out is not the worst of it. The worst of it is that the finding will be true, and it will be useless, and it will be in a book, and everybody in this city will be able to point at it and say *that is on the record* and it will not be on the record of anything at all, because the only instrument that could have acted on it is the one you cannot get a name for.

"That is the whole of what a room can do. I have been nineteen years in this work and that is the whole of what a room can do, and I would like it noticed that I have said it as a professional and not as a complaint, because if I say it as a complaint then I am a person in a doorway having a bad morning, and I have met two of those this year and one of them is standing in this room."

---

Seren Calder stood at the rail for a moment with her hands on it.

"Then I will have it entered," she said, "and I will say the thing nobody in this room wants to hear and which I have been saying for two months in four rooms and on a bank and at the foot of a ladder.

"The emergency is real. In the third month the low ground goes under, and I am right about that, and I have been right about that every day since the sixteenth of the first month and I have never once been wrong about it, and nobody in this kingdom is going to make me be wrong about it in order to be right about anything else.

"And you have not answered me, and you were not able to, and I am not going to walk out of here and say that anybody answered me.

"Produce it at me," she said, to Deputy Tolek. "In four years, when the man in the good coat comes. Produce the finding and I will be in the room, and I will stand there and let him read it out, and I will not have read the price box then either, and he can put that in a file as well."

"That is not a very good idea," said Deputy Tolek.

"It is a first-rate idea," said Seren Calder, "and it is the first one I have had in two months and I am going to take it and you may write down that I said so."

---

Deputy Tolek read the finding out loud twice at the ordinary speed, and it was a quarter of a page, and it said that a challenge was brought on the tenth of the third month of MR 416 by an officer of the Crown March; that the object of it was a requirement and not a sheet; that the court heard it and had no power to make a policy; that the court could not refuse a challenge and had not done so; and that the court had looked for a way to act on the matter and had not found one, and was not going to invent a finding to fill the place of one.

And it went into a book on the causeway side of a hall with six intake windows, and it has a day on it, and it is the only document in this basin in about a year with a day on it that says an instrument cannot do what it is for, and nine or ten people in a doorway heard a marshal say that the emergency was real and not one word of it was disputed, and nobody in that doorway or in that room was thanked for anything, and the boy of about nine went down the causeway with a message he had not been asked about, and the low ground was nine days off going under.
