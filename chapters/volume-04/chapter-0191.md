# Chapter 191 — The Four, and the Reason for Each

The queue at the rail on the causeway side does not stop between the fourth bell and the fifth, and a woman of fifty-one who has been behind that counter for thirty years knows to the length of a body how long it takes to find a gap in it, and she did not use the one she was standing in.

She used a woman of forty-four with a pen who had been in the corridor for about an hour and a half with nothing to write on.

"Deputy," said Perpetua Ohl. "You have been out there since the third bell with an empty sheet and I would like you to have something to put on it before the fifth, because whatever it is has to be said while the queue is on the causeway and not afterwards in a room with a door."

"I have been out there," said Deputy Vera Tolek, "because a hearing officer cannot do this on a causeway, and I have been standing in a corridor working out how to do it on a causeway, and you have just solved it in a sentence, and I would like it noted that you have solved it by telling me the thing I was not allowed to do."

"I have told you the thing you were not allowed to do," said Perpetua Ohl, "and you are going to do it, and I am going to write it out, and neither of us is going to sign it, and I would like both of those settled before anybody starts."

---

"Say the no-signing first," said Deputy Tolek.

"I will not stand behind a piece of paper," said Perpetua Ohl. "That is not modesty and I want it understood that it is not modesty. I have entered other people's words every working day of my adult life and I have never once been asked to stand behind one, and in the fourth month of this year a woman carried a slate to this counter and asked me for a day on a painted shutter and I refused her in three reasons, and one of them was that a shutter is not a page.

"And in the fourth month of this year a man with no office put a clerk's name at the foot of a man's own sheet in a room in a lane, and he did it because a boy of about twenty-nine is entitled to ask his father a question, and he said the four reasons out loud before the pencil moved.

"I have not stopped hearing about it. I am not going to start in the fifth month of a year in which the only clerk in this basin has put his name at the bottom of a page for a reason that was good, and I have not got the standing to do it and neither have you."

"I have got the standing to enter a finding and not to publish one," said Deputy Tolek. "That is a real distinction and it is in the rules and nobody has ever needed it before. Go on."

"Then here is the other half," said Perpetua Ohl, "and it is the half that matters, and I have been keeping it since the sixth month of last year and I have not been thanked for it and I am not going to be now.

"There are four instruments. Four documents, made in this basin in the last year, and I am not giving you a fifth and there is not a fifth that I have found, and I would like the finding to say four and not about four, because four is a count of pieces of paper and four is the only number in this business anybody is allowed to say out loud.

"And I am not going to name a person on any of them."

---

The Deputy wrote two words at the head of the sheet and then stopped and looked at them.

"There are four names in a book in this building," she said, "that a woman of fifty-one read out on the seventh of the first month of this year to nine people, and they are the four that this volume's worst thing is built on, and I have been asked twice this morning whether these four are those four, and I am going to answer the question before anybody asks it a third time.

"They are not. There are four of you in a book and there are four of these, and nobody has put the two fours together and nobody is going to, and I am putting that in the finding in those words because the next person who comes down this causeway is going to want them to be the same four, and there is nothing in the world to stop him wanting that except a sentence somebody wrote down first."

"You have got a room behind a Threshold that takes challenges for free and will not refuse one," said Perpetua Ohl. "You have got a list of four people in a book and a list of four papers on a sheet. Write both down and see what a man in a good coat does with them in about four years."

"I have written them down," said Deputy Tolek.

---

She read it out on the causeway side, at the rail, at about a quarter past the fifth bell, in the voice she uses in a room, which is slower than anybody expects.

**"One. A works order on the low-ground road, carried in the fourth month of MR 416, on a line of district accounts. The dangerous thing about it is not what it does. The dangerous thing about it is that it is a season, and it falls on the two ends of a border, and the two ends of a border are two households, and neither household was asked."**

**"Two. A contract of supply against the clinic, sealed in the second month of MR 416, witnessed at the eighth bell by a person of record. The dangerous thing about it is that it was witnessed by a person who had already been produced, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a person may be produced twice, so the witnessing on it is the second reading of a first one, and it is not a witnessing, and there is no box on any paper anywhere that can say the difference."**

**"Three. A lease on a pitch on the low-ground road, up at the end of the fourth month, witnessed in a room with no employer in it. The dangerous thing about it is that a paper witnessed in a room with no employer is good in that room and nowhere else, and the lease is a paper about a pitch on a public road, and the moment anybody in this basin wants that lease to be good in a district office, the room is not where it happens in."**

**"Four. An indentures of four years, witnessed at a counter in the ninth month of MR 412 by a person of record. The dangerous thing about it is that it is the only one of these four that is still doing something to somebody today, and it has about two years to run on it, and it is on a boy who was fourteen when it was signed and is eighteen now, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a person may be entered as saying they did not agree to a thing for four years, and there never has been one."**

She stopped, and looked up the causeway, and about eleven people had stopped on it, which was more than she had expected and fewer than she had hoped for.

"There is no name on any of those four," said Deputy Tolek. "There is no name on this sheet and there is not going to be one, and I am going to say why in nine sentences, and then I am going to read the sheet into the finding and the finding goes into a book in this building and stays there as long as the building stands."

---

"A name on a piece of paper is a person standing behind it," said Perpetua Ohl, from behind the counter, to nobody, in the way she has said things for thirty years.

"That is the whole of it and I am not going to improve on it. The four people whose papers these are are people this basin has already spent. There is no form in this kingdom on which a person may be produced twice, so a name on a public list is not a name on a public list, it is a hand that has been used a second time, and the people whose names would go on it are the people who can least afford it, because they were already in the ground when the question was asked.

"And there is a second reason and it is the reason I have kept for six months and have not been thanked for once.

"There is a price of fourteen attestations in a book a night and a day up this river, and it is a district figure and there is no return, because a return is a thing you make about a person and there is not a person on the entry. And there are about nine attestations of record in this basin. The difference between the two is five, and it has been five for six months and it is five this morning, and I am not going to add it to anything in the hearing of nine people on a causeway, and neither is she, and if anybody in this queue would like to make it four because there are four on the sheet, they may do it in their own head and keep it there, and I have not got a form on which a person may be told they are wrong."

Somebody at the back of the queue said the word *nonsense*, quietly, and it was not answered, and it was not ignored either.

---

"I have four of them told," said Perpetua Ohl, "and I have kept them in a book in a drawer under this counter for six months and have not read them out for anybody, and I am not going to read them out now.

"One of them found out in a rail queue and has been asked nine times since and not one question. One of them was told in her own room. One of them was told at a rail and went back to his count. One of them is at a coast and was told by a carter's mouth and has not come back. A fifth has not been told and there is no form in this kingdom on which anybody may be told anything, and a sixth has been dead since the year before last and never knew, and I entered all of it and I have never been thanked for any of it and I am not going to be thanked for this."

"Then say the thing the sheet is for," said Deputy Tolek. "You have not said it."

"The sheet is for the fifth thing," said Perpetua Ohl, "and the fifth thing is this. A list of four documents with the reason each one is dangerous is worth a great deal to about nine hundred people on this causeway a day, and it is worth nothing at all in a book in a building, because in four years a man in a good coat produces a book in a building at a clerk in a good coat and the clerk reads it to the person it is about and the person has no way of answering.

"I want it read out on a causeway where nine hundred people walk past it, and I want the reading to be a thing people can say they heard, and not one of those nine hundred can prove a single word of it, and that is the arrangement and it is the only one on offer."

---

The Deputy read the sheet into the finding, twice, at the ordinary speed, and signed it, and the finding says that the sheet is a true copy of the sheet and that the sheet is a true copy of a document and not a copy of anybody, and that no person is named in it and that the omission was made deliberately and is not an omission, and that the two lists of four in this basin do not touch and have not been joined by anybody in this room or in this city.

And then a boy of eighteen came up to the rail, and he had been in the queue the whole time, and he was the fourth of the four in a way that none of the four documents was.

"I have got the third of them," he said. "Not the lease. The one before it. I held the pitch off my mother's account for two seasons and the lease went up in the third month of this year and I have not been able to do anything with it since and I have come here about a bin, and not about the bin, but about a box at a counter where a woman told me in a way I could not argue with that there is no form on which anybody may be refused, and I have been carrying that round for two months."

"You have got the third of them," said Deputy Tolek, "and I am not going to put your name on this sheet, and I am going to read the third of it again to you slowly, because you have heard it once at the other end of this causeway at the fifth bell and you were one of eleven people, and hearing it twice is not a formality. It is the only instrument in this business that anybody has ever been able to improve on."

She read it again. He listened to it with his hands on the rail.

"It is not wrong," he said, when she had finished. "That is the thing I have been waiting two months for somebody to say and four people have said the other one. It is not wrong that the lease is not good anywhere. It is not wrong that I cannot stand in that room twice, or that my mother cannot be asked, or that there is nobody to ask because everybody in it has been used.

"And I have got two seasons of a pitch and a rate on my mother's account that nobody has ever said out loud, and I am eighteen, and in about two years the fourth of the four on that sheet is going to be finished, and I would like somebody to say one thing to me and I have not got anybody to say it to, and it is not *are you free*, because that is the one nobody has ever printed."

Deputy Tolek put her pen down flat on the desk and did not pick it up for a while.

"Then say the thing you want and it will go in a finding with a day on it and nobody will be able to take the day off," she said, "and I am not going to refuse it, and I have not, and I will not."

"You are owed something," said the boy, "and you have a word for it, because you are a hearing officer and you have got one on a form, and I have got two seasons of a pitch and a rate on my mother's account and I am eighteen, and I want to be able to say out loud what it is, so that somebody in a room in four years has to read the same words."

"There is no form in this kingdom on which a person may be entered as saying they did not agree to a thing for four years," said Deputy Tolek, "and I entered that this morning in a finding on a causeway, and I am going to enter the other one in your words, and it is going to say that the instrument does not test how it was obtained, and that the name is the name, and that there is no box.

"And I am entering a third thing on my own initiative, at this counter, with nobody asking me, and it is a sentence I have not written down before and I have been in this work nineteen years.

"The thing that has no name on a form is not that you were not free. You were not free. Everybody in this business knows it and I have never been able to write it. The thing that has no name on a form is that you cannot be asked, ever again, by anybody, about anything, ever, and there is no form on which that may be repaired, and that is the part, and I have been in four rooms in four months where the rooms were full of people who had all of their names taken off them in a way nobody intended."

She wrote it out and read it back and signed it and put the finding in a book in a building on the causeway side, where it will be as long as the building stands.

And the four documents are named, and the reason for each is named, and no person is named, and a boy of eighteen walked away down the causeway with a piece of paper in his own hand that nobody had written for him and that nobody was going to countersign, because he did not need one and had not been asked for one, and it is a true copy of a true copy and it is good for nothing except that it is true.

Nobody at that counter was thanked for anything, and four of the five of the people it was about did not know it had happened, and the fifth was at a coast.
