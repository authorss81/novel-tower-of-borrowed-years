# Chapter 196 — What It Comes Back To

Deputy Tolek set one aside on the fourteenth of the fifth month of MR 416 at about the fifth bell, in the second of the four rooms behind the Threshold, which is ten feet by eight, and it took her about eleven seconds, and it is the first time in this basin that an instrument has been set aside by anybody, and she did it on her own authority and nobody asked her to and there is no form on which she could have been asked.

The instrument is a contract of supply against the clinic, sealed in the second month of this year, witnessed at the eighth bell by a person of record. The dangerous thing about it, on a causeway on the second of the month, in front of about nine hundred people a day, was that the witness had already been produced, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a person may be produced twice, so the witnessing on it is a second reading of a first one and not a witnessing at all.

She put it face down on a shelf by the window. There is no other shelf in that room. It is an open room and there are nine or ten people in a corridor outside it and anybody may come in and look at the paper on the shelf, and it is the only instrument in this kingdom in that position, and she wrote nothing on it and put nothing over it and did not tie it up.

"There," she said. "That is the whole of the act and it has cost me nothing, and I want to say why it has cost me nothing while there are still people in the corridor, because it is going to sound like a thing I did for a reason.

"I was produced on the twelfth of this month, at a counter, in the ordinary way, on a form with five lines on it, by my own hand, and I told the room the cost before the pen was near it, and I said in this room ten days ago that not one finding I had entered in two years had cost the person it was about anything, and I have wanted one of them to cost somebody something since the fourth of the first month.

"And then I became the person it is about. So I have got a finding that costs, and I am not going to pretend the finding is what did it. The finding is the finding. I am the instrument. A spent witness who sets a paper aside is a thing that could have happened to anybody and it happened to me on purpose and I chose it, and I have not been thanked for it and I would rather nobody did, because I chose it and choosing is not being owed."

---

The paper does not come back to anybody, and the reason is a form, and the form is the finding of the fourth of the first month of this year in a book in a building on the causeway side, and it has a day on it.

It says that a court cannot enter a finding that any person did or did not consent to anything, and that it has looked, and there is none to enter.

"There is no box," said Deputy Tolek. "I have said this in a room before and I am saying it in a corridor now and it is the same sentence and I am not going to improve it.

"There is no box on that form, in this printing or in any printing anybody in this kingdom has ever had, on which a person is asked whether they were free. There never has been one. And the thing nobody has put together, and I have been waiting five months for somebody to put together, is that the same absence takes the coming back.

"A paper coming aside brings a witness back. That is the rule and it is the only rule in this business that anybody ever described as good, and it works, and it has worked for nineteen years, and a person comes back when the paper stops.

"But a paper coming aside only un-spends a person if somebody asks them. That is the whole of it. Being un-spent is not a thing that happens to a person. It is a thing that happens to a person when somebody asks them a question and they answer it, and the asking has to be done by somebody, and there is no form in this kingdom on which anybody may be asked to do it, and the only party who could do it is the office that produced them, and that office has a rule of its own, and the rule is that a person of record who has been produced may not be approached a second time about anything.

"You cannot put back what you never had a box for. That is the sentence. I have had it in a drawer in my own head since the fourth of the first month and I have not said it out loud in a room until now, and I am not going to say it a second time, and if it is in a finding anywhere then I would like the finding struck, because I did not authorise it."

---

Four people had been told, and the four are these.

**Zlata Hruska, thirty-four**, was told in her own room on the second of the second month of this year and she was in it when the paper came off the shelf on the causeway side, because a boy of about nine had carried eleven words up a ladder and no reason, and she read them out and put them down and did not go down to the second room and did not ask what instrument it was.

"I am not going to come down and look at it," she said. "That is the whole of what I have got to say about it and I would like it said in whatever it is you are making that I said it before anybody thought to ask me.

"And I want the true thing said as well and I want it said about me and not about the room, because people will get this wrong and they will get it wrong in my direction.

"I was produced twice before anybody told me and I found out in this room in one go in the second month and I was the first person in this basin to be told the whole of it in one room in one go, and there are two other people in this basin who know they have been produced and neither of them has been asked about it here, and I have never once been asked about it by anybody either, and the reason I have not been asked is that the room asked and the room did not require, and I was one of the four bodies in it and nobody made me a chair.

"And a paper coming off a shelf does not ask me anything. That is what a shelf is. I have been on a wall in chalk since the twenty-seventh of the first month and a man can rub my name off it, and nobody has, and a day is written against my line and any person can walk along that wall and say that is the twenty-ninth and be right or be wrong in front of the other nine, and that is a shelf with a day on it. I had rather have that than a bench in a room and I am not going to be moved off it by a sheet of paper in a room ten feet by eight."

**Drazen Topic, thirty-four**, was told at a market rail on the ninth of the second month, went back to his count, and was on the rail at the eighth bell on the fourteenth of the fifth month, and a man with no office came and stood at the end of it and did not interrupt a queue.

"You are going to ask me whether I want to know," he said. "You are going to ask me whether I want to be asked again, and I am going to tell you before you get there that I have got a slate in my hand and nothing on it and a day written against my name on a wall in a lane, and that between those two things there is exactly one question left in my life that anybody can ask me, and it is the one you are going to ask, and I have answered it four times in four months.

"Ask it, then."

"How many days," said Marek Vale.

"About six," said Drazen Topic, "and it is not the same six every time and nobody writes it down and that is the arrangement."

"Then it is still the same six," said Marek Vale, and said nothing else, and did not ask a second question, and did not write it down, and went and stood nine feet off the end of the rail where he was not in anybody's way.

**Odile Marn, thirty-one**, was told in a rail queue in the sixth month of last year and has been asked nine times since and not one question, and on the fourteenth of the fifth month of this year she was at the door of the seed house off the Counters' Walk at about the second bell with a spade and a strip of label tape round the shaft and a man of fifty-two behind her in the doorway with his coat off.

"I have had another look at the wall," she said. "It is the wall I wanted mended. It was mended in the growth month of MR 411 and it is the only thing I ever got out of that form, and nine feet of the course on the near side has not run since the winter and is going to come down in the rains and is going to come down on the ditch.

"So I have had a man look at it who does that, and he says it wants doing properly and by the second month of next year, and he has given me a figure and I have got the money out of my own hand over a season, and I am going to have it done in the ninth month and I am not going to have anybody stand and say they did it.

"And that is not a brave thing and I am not going to be told it is. A form about a wall would be a paper about a wall, and a wall is not a line and a line is not a paper, and if somebody stood up and said I did it then in about four years a man in a good coat could produce that at me and at the man and at both our households over one and a half feet of brick, and there are two children in this house.

"So I have said the whole of it out loud on a bank of clay with two people standing on it and neither of them has written it down, and you are one of them, and I would like it noticed that I did not ask either of you to."

Nobody noticed it except the two of them.

**Jelka Sondor, twenty-six**, is at a coast and has not come back and cannot be reached by anything in this basin that is not a four-hour road, and the charter needs a witness.

"There is no finding I can enter about her," said Deputy Tolek, "and I am not going to pretend that is a difficulty in the room. There is a finding I can enter and it is a finding about a person at a coast and it says that she was told on the twenty-sixth of the third month by a carter's mouth, and that a carter's mouth is not a form, and that no office in this kingdom has any hold on a woman at the end of a road, and that none of the four of them may be told anything further, and the finding is true and it is worth nothing and I am entering it because I have entered four hundred true things that were worth nothing and that is what this work is."

---

The water came off the Rainmarket low ground in the fifth month of MR 416, and nobody wrote down the day it happened on, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a person may be told what day a thing happened on, and it came off the way she said it would come off, and the foot and a half of new clay was underneath it and hard under a boot, and a mile and a bit of it.

Neris Kade and Marek Vale walked the Counters' Walk on the fifteenth of the fifth month, from the seed house to the far end, with a stake and a maul out of a store on the low-ground road that a man of fifty lends out and does not ask about, and four hundred yards of new trench cut between the twenty-sixth and the twenty-eighth of the second month.

Kade counted the holdings on both sides of it. She took two days over it in the fourth month and could not do it because of the water, and she did it in about three hours on the fifteenth of the fifth month because there was a bank to stand on.

"There are eleven," she said. "On the line. Six on the near side, five on the far, and the near end is a man of fifty who keeps a key and has a thumb on a piece of paper, and the far end is a woman of sixty-two with a good yard that was her mother's, and there is a person between them at each end who is not on either list and I have not put their names in my head and I am not going to."

She wrote a period in her column that evening and a period about a ditch and a water going off and nothing else, and read it out loud twice at the ordinary speed, because that is what a person does with a sheet in a flat case, and the line at the foot with nothing in it since the seventh month of last year is still blank and neither of them named it then and neither of them named it now.

"Eleven is a number about a line," said Marek Vale.

"Yes," said Neris Kade. "And a number about a line is a figure about a district, and a district is a line in a book in this city, and the day that number is in my column it is in a sheet in a flat case with two names at the head of it and in nine months a man in a good coat can produce this sheet at either of us.

"So it is not going in. And I am not going to pretend I have not got it, because I have got it and I counted it and I know it, and I have been walking this basin for three years telling people that the thing that happened to them is not their own fault, and I have never once in three years been the person who decided what somebody was going to carry, and I am not going to start on a morning in the fifth month with a boot on a good bank.

"And if we do not put it down, somebody in an office puts it down out of a book, with a head on it, and it is wrong in a way nobody can check, and it lands on somebody who did not agree to be landed on, and there is nothing about that number that anybody in this basin could have stopped.

"That was your half in the fourth month, word for word nearly, and it is still true, and it has not got any smaller because there is a boot under my foot and I can see both sides of the line."

"Neither has mine," said Marek Vale. "It has not got any smaller because you can count it. I would rather she were never counted than counted by a person she cannot see, in a column she cannot read, on a sheet with my name at the head of the other side of it. And there is no form in this kingdom on which anybody may be told that a bill is coming, and it is the fifth month, and she is at the top of that road with her hand on a latch, and she has still not been told, and neither of us is going to tell her, and neither of us is going to go up the road and ask her whether it is a good yard again."

So there is a number about a line, and it is in a woman's head, and it is in no column and on no page and in no form, and two people know it, and it is not going to be in any of them, and the fourth chair up the ladder is empty and is her name and stays empty, and the low ground is dry, and the ditch is still cut four hundred yards from one good yard to a man with a key, and neither end of it has been told anything.
