# Review — Volume 02, Batch 0001 (Chapters 51–60)

Reviewer pass on the finished batch, followed by a fix pass over the prose and the artefacts.
**Prose is authoritative.** Where a card, a state file or a prompt disagreed with a chapter,
the chapter won and the artefact was corrected. No planned plot movement was changed, no
chapter was restarted, and no scene was cut or padded.

## Verdict

Ten finished scenes, 24,933 words inside the 24,000–28,000 plan, an even length distribution,
one Ledger Sight panel used correctly and alone, no Boundary Hold, no manufactured villain, and
the volume's thesis carried in five registers without a lecture. **The batch stands.** What was
wrong was bookkeeping, and bookkeeping in this repository propagates: the artefacts the *next*
phase reads were wrong in ways that would have been inherited as fact.

## Findings, and what was done

### Blocking — the batch 0002 prompt was wrong and would have scoped the writer wrongly

1. **`PROMPT.md` called Movement II "the last third of the volume."** Volume 02 is five movements
   of ten chapters and five batches. Chapters 61–70 are the second of the five. Corrected, with
   the two remaining movements and the two remaining batches named.
2. **`PROMPT.md` said four batches follow this one.** Three do: 0003, 0004, 0005. Corrected.
3. **`PROMPT.md` and `state/current.md` said three people know the chain, naming Ostrowsky.**
   `Ostrowsky` occurs zero times in Chapters 56–60 and the Chapter 60 scene is two people: Kelb
   tells Marek and leaves. **Two know it — Kelb, who has told nobody, and Marek.** Corrected in
   the prompt, `current.md` (two places) and `open-threads.md`, and recorded in `continuity.md`
   so the error cannot come back through a third file.
4. **Merrow's history.** Chapter 54 has him say *in nine years I have made two offers*; the two
   *four years* in that chapter are the four years it took him to put the difficulty into one
   sentence. `state/current.md` carried the four-year version and is corrected; the prompt now
   states the nine years and the two refused offers outright. `open-threads.md` and
   `continuity.md` had it right and were checked rather than changed.
5. **Orleb's assignment was dated MR 411.** Chapter 55 has Ferrow call it *eight months old* on
   the sixth of the seed month of MR 413, which is the second month of **MR 412**; MR 411 also
   predates the instrument the assignments travel with. Corrected in the prompt, `continuity.md`,
   `open-threads.md`, the Chapter 55 summary and the Chapter 55 card.

### Prose defects

6. **Chapter 60's closing exchange had Sedge say "Go and count your other eighteen" twice,
   verbatim, four lines apart, in the same conversation** — and the figure was impossible
   (Chapter 57 has seventeen files still on the list on the eighth, and the fourth is one of
   them). The first instance now ends on the list behind Marek with a line on it that is not
   done; the second ends on Sedge saying he will want somebody to be angry at. **No total of
   files finished is stated in the batch or in any state file, by design**, and the next writer
   is told so in three places.
7. **Chapter 60 gave Sedge a service length twice** ("in nineteen years", "on this hill for
   nineteen years"). Nineteen belongs to Vendl, Grale and Ostrowsky, and `current.md` had locked
   that Sedge has none. Both instances are gone; he now says he read the clause *until this
   morning* and has been on the hill *long enough that twice in it*.
8. **Chapter 58's arithmetic: forty hands up, nineteen of sixty-one not in the room.** 40 + 19 is
   59. **Twenty-one.** The same bad figure had been copied into `open-threads.md`.
9. **Chapter 53 said the margin sentence was twenty-two words and then, eleven lines later,
   eleven.** It is twenty-two, and now says so twice. The state file had recorded this as fixed
   when it was not — see finding 17.
10. **Chapter 51 said "Three days after a board named it."** The board wrote the word *Tallow*
    beside the 40,900 on the seventeenth of the rains month; the letter is dated the second of the
    seed month. **Sixteen days**, and the month length that makes that true is now fixed in
    `continuity.md` (the rains month has thirty-one days, which is what Chapter 51's own "nine
    days ago the reckoning had turned" requires).
11. **Chapter 58 said the fourth clause was four years old.** It was sealed on the ninth day of
    the bad autumn, MR 411. **Eighteen months.**
12. **Minor, all fixed.** Chapter 52's *There is one more and he is in the loft* read as a
    sixty-second member and now reads *He is in the twenty*. Chapter 60's notice-reader count now
    thins on the page — about nine where there had been thirty the week before — instead of
    drifting silently from Chapter 54. Chapter 53's *on a Thursday afternoon* asserted a weekday
    the calendar cannot support and now reads *on an ordinary afternoon*. **Ilar Betz**, a
    one-scene market factor, is recorded in `continuity.md`. **Ostrowsky is no relation of the
    Ostrow family** and the resemblance may not be used or explained; recorded as a live trap.

### State-file accuracy

13. "`Chapters 51–60 do not describe, refer to, or look at the mark'" was false: Chapter 53 names
    *a mark on his forearm* in Marek's own private arithmetic. Referring is not describing and
    the guardrail holds; the sentence now says what is true, in `current.md` and `continuity.md`.
14. "The certificate is referred to twice in dialogue" was false: the word *certificate* is used
    once, by Merrow in Chapter 54. Chapter 60 names the finding. Corrected in both files.
15. "`No weekday is stated in Chapters 51–60`" was false — four weekday words appear. Three are
    habitual or predictive; the fourth asserted the day of the scene and has been removed.
    `continuity.md` now records the month-length canon, the Volume 01 anchor (**the ninth of the
    rains is a Wednesday**), and the rule for counting, and says plainly that the batch
    establishes no weekday.
16. "A promise to his brother" was wrong twice: the promise is to his mother, at her door, and it
    runs the other way — he had said he would come and cannot remember saying it. Corrected in
    `continuity.md` and the Chapter 53 summary. **`outline/volume-02.md` still says "brother" in
    its Chapter 53 map line and has deliberately not been edited**, because it is the volume's
    authoritative plan and batch 0002's own prompt forbids editing it; the correction lives in
    the continuity file, which that prompt tells the writer to read first.
17. **Two contradictory self-reports of the same pass** — "fixed eight continuity defects" and
    "found and fixed twenty-two" — one with a malformed bullet, in a file whose own text warns
    that a self-reported pass is not evidence. They are now one accurate paragraph that also
    records that items (6)/(7) and (22) had been claimed closed while still open in the prose.
18. **`reviews/` held no report for any batch and `logs/` is gitignored**, so the quality gate was
    being met by an untracked file. **This report is the tracked artefact.**

## What was working, and was not touched

Panel discipline is exact — one panel, Chapter 53, alone on a landing, plain language, showing an
absence rather than fabricating one, cost paid in a lost small memory. No hold, no wishing for
one. Merrow, Ferrow, Vey and Pike each stay honest and each costs the reader something; the moral
work is done by a form with a missing box. The romance does not move, and what landed instead was
Kade naming the shape of his danger in Chapter 52 and paying for it in Chapter 60. Chapters 55
and 56 are the strongest in the batch. The structural note — 53 and 60 both at a long table, 56
also at a long table — is real, is recorded in `continuity.md`, and was passed forward to the
batch 0002 prompt as the engine to avoid rather than repaired by cutting a good scene.
