# Volume 02 Batch 0001 Outline — What the Share Signed

Batch number: 0001
Chapter range: 51–60
Batch title: **What the Share Signed**
Expected length: **24,000–28,000 words across ten chapters.** Ordinary chapters run 2,400–2,900 words. **Chapter 53 and Chapter 60 may run to about 3,000 because they are the batch's two practice chapters**, and Chapter 54 may run long because Ovid Merrow's first scene has to be the best-argued scene in the book so far. **Never pad to reach a number and never cut a complete scene to hit one.** Volume 01 ran about 4,500 words over its own ceiling and the finding is recorded as unresolved in `state/current.md`; **the ceiling here is advisory, and if you overrun, record the honest figure in `state/current.md` and do not round it down.**

Batch goal: Make the reader understand, concretely and without a lecture, **what a cooperative seed loan is, what an assignment is, what a counterweight is, and what it costs when a district is named where a person should be.** Introduce Ovid Merrow as a man the reader half agrees with and cannot refute. Put the moral cost in a kitchen where no clerk is present and nobody lies. Deliver the batch's midpoint reversal — **the whole stack balances and there is no lie in it** — and name the fourth kind of entry out of arithmetic, in a room, by a character. End on a hand copy in the open that Marek is not allowed to enter in anything, a chain of nine links, and a decision to recertify nothing.

Starting condition: **MR 413, the first month of the year, the seed month, the second.** The reckoning turned nine days ago. The rains have not done anything for a month and the Bell Weir is at its limit. The four Tallow yards are running on the public levy; the wage comes out of a reserve carry of **40,900** that has been entered every year since the flood years and was drawn on for the first time three centuries into its life by a board, with a district's name beside it. The school is shut. Tomas Vale is on the north-road span. Lio is off the roll. Hesper Rann is in Crown March. Renata Vole is in the coastal returns. The Trust's sealed notice is on the Tally Hall door and cannot be carried out by anybody. The public register has a new page in it with a blank line left blank and a signature written out in full under it.

Starting relationships: **Marek has no office, no standing, no form, and no work.** He is a clerk of the Chamber. The letter *V.* is not back on the assignment column and nobody explained it and he did not ask. **Neris Kade distrusts his access and has said so; she is Rainmarket's examiner this season and the Share is Rainmarket's season work.** Ovid Merrow has not met him. Ondra Kelb is at a desk with a left hand. Tomas and Tamsin are unchanged and no better.

Batch shape: **Beginning** (51–52) → **setup and the mechanism** (53–54) → **the moral cost in a kitchen** (55) → **midpoint** (56) → **escalation** (57–59) → **climax and decision** (60) → **coda** (close of 60).

Batch midpoint: **Chapter 56.** Ondra Kelb's counting floor, the eighth bell, and the discovery that the Compact's bundle balances to the penny. Every rate, every assignment, every date and every signature in the stack is permitted by a rule that predates the Compact. There is no false term, no omitted page, no invented reserve, no aimed chain, and nothing in it that anybody may be shown to have done wrong. The batch's opening belief — *I have found the lie, and a lie is contestable* — breaks against arithmetic, not against a person. **Kelb is ashamed of how much he is relieved, and says so.**

Batch climax: **Chapter 60.** The last evening before the seed goes into the ground. Marek makes a longhand copy of the Share's instrument and the assignment schedule in the open, with his name and no office at the foot and another person counting with him, and finds the chain: **nine links, the second from the end a Tallow Gate carrier's arrears, the last a district.** He is not permitted to enter it in the public register, because the register's page is the Trust's page and a man with no office may enter nothing. He decides to recertify nothing, and he says so in person to the man who gave him the list, which he has never done in his life.

Aftermath: the tail of Chapter 60, after the copy room, four paragraphs. **Nim Rook** comes and stands in the room because there has been nothing to carry for a month, and Marek tells her he has nothing, and she says the price was never the information.

**Locked instruments (from `outline/volume-02.md`, do not blur):**

- **The Share's seed instrument.** Cooperative, sealed once for sixty-one members, all five parts present, **two witnesses neither of whom is paid by the Share**, borrowed **2,400** in the bad autumn of MR 411, pledged as next season's labour of eleven days each plus a third of the seed kept back, scoped to the seed house and the low ground, settled out of the harvest. **Its fourth clause recites a counterweight: the district named in the schedule bears the failure of the season favoured.** The district named in the schedule is the Rainmarket low ground. **This clause is correct, well drafted, and has been sealed for four years. No chapter in this batch may find a defect in the Share's own instrument.**
- **The assignment schedule.** Fifty-two lines out of sixty-one, dated, signed by the assignor, **no rate box, no name box for whoever the rate is charged to**, and the form predates the practice. Nine members have no arrears and did not sign. Total **1,916**.
- **The bundle, or the paper.** The Share's instrument plus the fifty-two assigned claims, sold as one line at **300**, carrying Halvard Sunn's Tide Reckoning counterseal, with a stated rate and an unstated counterweight.
- **The recertification.** A second clerk's eye, due back on the **twelfth**, which is the day the seed goes in. Twenty-two files. Marek is on the list because he is the only clerk in Bell Stair with nothing to do.

**Locked figures:** sixty-one members; fifty-two assigned; nine not; 2,400 borrowed; 1,916 assigned; 300 the paper's price; 1,100 the seed cost; 40 a second-hand plough; **nine links in the chain**; the carry is 40,900 and is due in the ninth month. **Do not make the assigned total and the bundle total agree. Do not make the chain sum to anything.**

**The batch's clock is ten days.** The second to the eleventh, and everything is lost on the twelfth. A seed that does not go into the ground in the seed month is a lost year, and no magic is involved.

### Chapter 0051 — Nine Months

POV: Marek.

Location/time: The levy office and the copy room, Bell Stair, **the second of the seed month, MR 413**, the first bell to the sixth.

Immediate goal: Find out what he is, now that the suspension slip has come down and the letter *V.* has not gone back on the assignment column.

Resistance: Nobody will tell him, and nobody is unkind about it. **Sedge** applies a rule correctly and it costs Marek a morning. **Ostrowsky**, nineteen years in the post, is the one who finally explains the column without being asked, and what he explains is that the column is where a clerk's name goes when a file is his, and Marek's name is not on it, and there is no form for a clerk who is employed and not assigned.

Information revealed, in this order and all of it small: **the levy office has received its first question about the reserve carry in three hundred years** — not a demand, a query, from the water office, asking the levy office to show the line and to say what it is against. Grale reads it and says what he has said in a yard, in the first person, and adds the new part: it is not a question about the money, it is a question about **whether there is a district on the line**, and a district is the only thing a question like that is for. And the second thing: **twenty-two files need a recertification before the twelfth**, because every seed instrument in the basin comes due in the same fortnight, and the fourth line on the list is the Elmsworth Seed Share's.

Action or decision: He takes the list. He does not ask to be given it and it is not given to him as a favour; it is put on a desk because the list exists and he is the only clerk at a desk with nothing on it. He reads the head of the fourth file and does not understand it and takes it anyway.

Emotional change: From guilt about a suspension that has already ended to the first dull, unfamiliar sensation of being useful, which is worse, because it is the thing he wants and there is no office attached to it.

Power or resource change: No gain and no panel. He gains a **deadline** and a stack of twenty-two files, and the volume's clock is the season's.

Continuity fact: The carry is due in the ninth month of MR 413. The recertification list is due back on the twelfth. Grale's horror is carried and not resolved and is **not** connected to anything in Rainmarket.

Ending type: a decision and a discovery of scale.

Next-chapter pull: The fourth line is a cooperative seed loan with sixty-one names on one seal, and there is a woman in Rainmarket who is going to tell him that usefulness is not standing before he has finished reading it.

### Chapter 0052 — The Man Who Is Useful

POV: Marek.

Location/time: The Elmsworth seed house and the low ground behind it, Rainmarket, **the third**, the sixth bell to dark.

Immediate goal: Find out what the Share is and what the fourth line of his list actually says, from somebody who is not paid by anybody in Bell Stair.

Resistance: **Mirren Fisk**, the seed-house keeper, will not speak to a man from Bell Stair and says so in the first thirty seconds. **Neris Kade** is there first, with a sheet she does not use, because she is Rainmarket's examiner this season and the Share is Rainmarket's season work, and she is not glad to see him. **Wenna Drel**, sixteen, is on the Share's roll because her mother is, and has drawn seed and has not voted, and nobody has explained to her that these are different things.

Information revealed: What a cooperative loan is, in the room where it is actually made — one seal, five parts, sixty-one members, a need, a pledge of next season's labour and a third of the seed kept back, a scope, two witnesses neither of whom is paid by the Share, a settlement out of the harvest. And the method, which is the volume's first real institutional detail: **the Share asks its members in a meeting and counts hands**, and Kade knows without being told that **a count of hands is the reason a cooperative can be sold** — a hand is a person in a room, and a person in a room can be asked, and the Compact is going to ask fifty-two of them individually over eight months and get the same fifty-two.

Action or decision: Marek asks the only question he can think of, which is whether the Share has read its fourth clause, and Fisk says the fourth clause is the threshing share, which means she has read the first three and not the fourth and has never been shown what the fourth does. He does not tell her. **He has no right to tell her and no form on which to say it, and he notices that this is the same wall as last year and that he is on the other side of it now.**

Emotional change: He is the person in the room who cannot speak. **Kade puts it in one sentence and it is the volume's thesis from her side:** *You have spent a month being a man with no office, and every room you walk into now is a room you got into by being useful. Useful is not standing, and somebody is going to take that off you at the worst possible moment.* He does not answer it and thinks about it for eleven days.

Power or resource change: None. He gains the Share's calendar — **the germination bench, the sowing date, the twelfth** — and the knowledge that a cooperative's consent is a count, which is the first half of the volume's method.

Continuity fact: The Share's witnesses are named as people: **a seed merchant's widow and a miller**, and neither is paid by the Share. Fisk's four columns and a bench that takes nine days to come up in the twelfth.

Ending type: an obstruction and a line that lands.

Next-chapter pull: The fourth clause is not the threshing share. Two clerks in Bell Stair have been sealing it for four years and neither of them has read it.

### Chapter 0053 — What a Counterweight Is For

POV: Marek.

Location/time: The copy room and the stair landing, Bell Stair, **the fourth**, the first bell to the sixth.

Immediate goal: Find out what a counterweight is, from the only two men in Bell Stair who have to know.

Resistance: **Ostrowsky** has been sealing seasonal instruments for eleven years and has never had to ask, because the question is only asked by somebody with no office. He is not hostile; he is **embarrassed**, and the embarrassment is the chapter. He tries three explanations and gets two of them slightly wrong, and the third one is right.

Information revealed, and it must be right and checkable: **a sequence loan moves the order of one narrow local event; a counterweight is the named future consequence that balances it; a counterweight may be named as a district, and a district is not a person, and there has never been a form on which a district consents.** The Share's fourth clause names the Rainmarket low ground, which is correct — it is the ground the seed is in — and the consequence of that correctness is that when the low ground's rate is charged, the cost sits on the sixty-one people who already paid. **Nothing is wrong with the clause. The clause is doing exactly what it says.** Then, alone, on the stair landing, the batch's one panel — an absence, not a fabrication. And the cost: a small recent ordinary memory, gone, and it is a promise to his brother.

Action or decision: He does not tell Ostrowsky what the Sight said. He asks instead to be allowed to walk the low ground, and Ostrowsky says the clerk of the day may not, and Marek says he is not the clerk of the day, and Ostrowsky says that is not a thing that gets you a walk. He goes anyway on the ninth.

Emotional change: He stops looking for a lie. Somewhere in the middle of the chapter he gives up the thing he came to Rainmarket to do and does not notice he has done it, and the reader notices before he does.

Power or resource change: **One Ledger Sight panel, alone, and a cost.** The Sight gives him a location and no evidence. It cannot look at a field and it cannot produce a missing entry; it can only tell him that the counterweight is named and that nothing is standing behind the name, and that is a true sentence about a file that is entirely proper.

Continuity fact: **Tamsin tells him at his door that he said he would come at the second bell and did not come, and she does not ask why, and he does not say.** She is not asked anything else in this batch.

Ending type: cost, and a place to go.

Next-chapter pull: On Rainmarket's side of the bridge there is a house over a chandler's shop where a man has been expecting a clerk with no office since the second.

### Chapter 0054 — Ovid Merrow's Morrowfen House

POV: Marek.

Location/time: The Sable Compact's Morrowfen house, over a chandler's shop, Rainmarket, **the fifth**, the second bell to the sixth.

Immediate goal: Be in a room with Ovid Merrow for two hours, because Kade said that was the only way to know what he was dealing with, and refuse to say anything she could not hear.

Resistance: **Ovid is the most articulate person Marek has ever been in a room with and he is not lying.** He has a licence, he pays, he discloses, and he says plainly that a district is not a person and was never one, and that the office which cannot ask a district has been handing him a district for nine years. He does not press. He offers Marek the two hours' worth of the method, in plain words, and Marek cannot interrupt him with a question because every question has already been answered in the sentence before it.

Information revealed, all of it in Ovid's own mouth and all of it true: what a bundle is; that the Compact's product is not credit but **explanation**; that the Trust cannot explain its own paper to anybody who is not a clerk and the Compact will; that he has tried for nine years to buy the oldest page in the city and cannot, because **a line with nothing in it has nobody standing behind it and you cannot buy from nobody** — and that in about nine months there will be a public register with a blank line on it and a name under it that is not a clerk's, and he will buy that in a morning. He cannot say what a signature is and does not pretend to.

Action or decision: Marek says the one thing he has, which is that the counterweight clause names a district and a district is not a person, and Ovid says yes, and says that the Share's clause is one of the best-drafted clauses he has ever read and that he would not have drafted it better, and that he did not draft it. **Marek learns that he has no answer, and that the honest thing about the room is that he lost it.**

Emotional change: He is not angry. That is the chapter's damage. He goes back over the causeway and finds he is arguing with himself about whether the man was right, and he loses.

Power or resource change: None, and none needed. The chapter is the batch's antagonist and its argument.

Continuity fact: Ovid is 47 and has run the Morrowfen house for nine years. He has never written down a thing that was not true. He is not a villain and must not be turned into one in the next five chapters.

Ending type: a reversal — the reader loses an argument it was winning.

Next-chapter pull: The offer is being made tonight, in kitchens, on barrels, by a woman called Ilse Ferrow, and the first household to be asked is a man of sixty-one with a second-hand plough.

### Chapter 0056 — It Balances

POV: Marek.

Location/time: Ondra Kelb's counting floor, the settlement desk, Bell Stair, **the seventh**, the eighth bell to the sixth.

Immediate goal: Find the error in the bundle before the twelfth, because if there is an error it can be contested and if there is not then Marek is wrong about the whole of Rainmarket.

Resistance: **Ondra Kelb** is the person doing the counting and Marek is not allowed to help. He is twenty-one, he has a hand-book on the wrong hand, and his own left hand does the fine work and is a nine-year-old's. He counts twice because that is the only thing he can do that other people cannot.

Information revealed: **The bundle balances to the penny.** Every rate, every assignment, every date, every witness and every signature in the stack is permitted by a rule that predates the Compact. There is no false term, no omitted page, no invented reserve, no aimed chain, and nothing in it that anybody can be shown to have done wrong. **Kelb says the sentence the chapter exists for: he has been a bad clerk for a year and he is the only one in the building who could find this, because he is the only one who counts twice.** And then he says that he has been **relieved**, and that the relief is shameful, and that he is saying it out loud because there is one other person in Bell Stair who would understand what it costs to be relieved by an absence of proof.

Action or decision: Marek does not argue with him, and does not comfort him, and does the second honest thing available: **he asks Kelb to say the number twice more, out loud, with a witness, so that the balance is a fact in a room and not a feeling in a man.** Kelb does. And then, from the same arithmetic and not from any file, **a name for the fourth kind**: an entry that is not a levy, a schedule, a posting or a return is an assignment, and there is no box for it because the form predates the practice.

Emotional change: The batch's belief breaks against arithmetic and not against a person. Marek's relief at *there is no lie* is as loud as Kelb's and he is more ashamed of it, because he came here to catch somebody.

Power or resource change: None. **The batch's centre of gravity moves from a document to a number spoken twice by two clerks with no standing.** No panel.

Continuity fact: The fourth kind is named here, out of arithmetic, by a character. **Hesper Rann's four dates are not touched in this batch and the fourth is still Grale's.**

Ending type: reversal, and a name.

Next-chapter pull: A grain factor has been in the building twice that week asking who in Bell Stair will read a piece of paper, and Marek has not noticed, because a man with nothing to do is a man people talk to.

### Chapter 0057 — The Price of a Market

Immediate goal: Understand why a grain factor wants the paper, from the man who wants it most.

Power or resource change: None. The batch gains the market's version of the same finding, from the other side.

### Chapter 0058 — A Shower in the Wrong Place

POV: Marek.

Location/time: The low ground and the Elmsworth bench, Rainmarket, **the ninth**, first light to dark, and a full day in a field.

Immediate goal: Look at the thing the Sight pointed at, with his own eyes, and find out what a counterweight does when nobody has been told.

Resistance: A field is twelve hours and nobody wants to be asked anything. **Kade works the way she worked Tallow Ward and Marek watches her do it and is not part of it.** **Sera Holt** is there, and the cost of a failed germination is a body's calendar and not a season's, and she is the one who says what nine failures do to a household that planned on sixty-one.

Information revealed: The Share's sequence clause favoured a narrow stretch on the sowing day and **the shower went to the high ground instead.** Nine of sixty-one samples failed. **Kade finds the one household that did not take the paper, and it is the household whose ground the shower went to, and she is pleased — and then she stops being pleased,** because the man who took the paper is Iren Orleb and the shower that missed is the same shower that will be needed. The mechanism is visible in the ground, in a bench, and in one woman's face, and nobody has done anything wrong.

Action or decision: Marek asks Kade what a counterweight is called when nobody is named, and she says a sentence that reframes the volume and is **not** a version of anything said in Volume 01: *It has been called a district for three hundred years. Somebody has to be the first person who says the word out loud in a room.* He does not say the word. He writes it on a sheet that is not a form.

Emotional change: He stops asking whether the Share's clause is a crime and starts asking whether a clause can be correct and fatal, which is a different and worse question and one he has no technique for.

Power or resource change: None on the page. **He gets what the panel promised: a location, an observation, and a witness who can be asked about it in a room** — which is the second cost arriving on schedule.

Continuity fact: **Do not connect the counterweight practice to Grale's cut-order suspicion.** A district-named counterweight is a current legal practice that predates the Compact. Grale's horror is about the cut order and about who wrote it sixty years ago, and it is not resolved in this volume.

Ending type: discovery in a body, in a field, on a bench.

Next-chapter pull: The Trust's seed credit window shuts on the sixth for a reason that is on paper and correct, and on the tenth there is a queue in the rain and a table in the lane with soup on it.

### Chapter 0059 — Fewer of Them Than in the Rains

POV: Marek.

Location/time: The Rainmarket Trust credit window and the lane outside it, **the tenth**, the first bell to dark, in rain.

Immediate goal: Find out whether the Compact's paper is being sold because people want it or because the alternative has stopped existing.

Resistance: **Rustam Vey** is correct. The window is shut for a reason on paper — the season's seed volume against the district account, and a rate nobody in the low ground can pay — and his rule is applied properly and costs four households a fortnight they did not have. He is not a villain and he is not available for argument. **Ferrow's table is in the lane, and it is orderly, and it is warm, and there is soup, and it is staffed by people who answer questions.**

Information revealed: The escalation. Several households take the paper on the tenth who would not have taken it on the fourth. **A closure by rule plus an offer on the other side of the door is not a conspiracy and it is not even dishonest; it is what a market does with a gap.** And the child: **Wenna Drel, sixteen, comes to the table and asks whether her name is on the paper, and is told yes, and is told that that means nothing, and signs on the line where her mother signs.**

Action or decision: **Kade does not intervene.** She stands eleven feet away and lets it happen, because an examiner may not speak to a party and because the girl is not a party, and because she knows what she is doing and does it anyway. That is the beat and it is the reader's indictment of her, not Marek's.

Emotional change: Marek does the arithmetic of the lane — how many people, how many papers, how many said no and how many said no because they were hungry and not because they understood — and he gets an answer and it is not a number he can use.

Power or resource change: None. **The batch's pressure is now in a lane with soup in it, which is the hardest place in the book to put an argument.**

Continuity fact: **Vey's rule is applied correctly and must not be discredited in this batch.** He is Volume 02's Aldis Kern, not its Hesper.

Ending type: pressure, and a restraint that costs somebody.

Next-chapter pull: The seed goes in on the twelfth, and twenty-two files are due back before it does, and Marek has not done a third of them and has read every one of them twice.

### Chapter 0060 — A Copy in the Open

POV: Marek.

Location/time: The copy room, Bell Stair, **the eleventh**, the fourth bell to the last of the light, the night before the seed goes in.

Immediate goal: Make a copy of the Share's instrument and the assignment schedule that somebody else can hold, because he is going to be asked to certify it in the morning and he has discovered that he does not know what the fourth clause is for.

Resistance: **A man with no office may read anything and certify nothing and enter nothing.** The public register's page is the Trust's page. The duty clerk lets him read a copy out, which is not the same as entering it, and she says so, and it is the only office she has. **The other person in the room is Ondra Kelb, whose left hand is better than the hand-book and is not his, and who counts with Marek because Marek cannot trust himself to count alone.**

Information revealed: The chain. **Nine links.** Fifty-two assigned claims from the low ground, then the low ground's district account, then the account's arrears bought whole by a house that cannot collect from a district, then a carrier's line at the Tallow Gate for hauling in the harvest, then a rate arrears of **40 and some months**, assigned, then the district again, and again, until the last link is a district and the second from the end is **one Tallow Gate household's arrears in a sum under fifty.** The chain is lawful at every step. **The last link is the ninth month, and the carry is due in the ninth month, and both of them end in Tallow Ward.**

Action or decision: He writes the date on the sheet and he goes home. **He does not go to Tallow Ward that night, and he says why to himself in one sentence: because he has no office, and a man with no office who turns up at a tank at midnight is a rumour, and in nine months the office will come to the tank and there will be a form for it.** **And in the morning he tells Sedge, in person, that he will not recertify the fourth file — not because it is false, and he says so, and Sedge believes him and it makes no difference — and he has never in his life told the man who gave him the work that he was not going to do it.**

Emotional change: He ends the batch with no work, no office, and the first thing he has done in a month that cost him something. **He is not a better man. He is a man who has found out that the only thing available to him is the one nobody can file.**

Power or resource change: No panel and no hold. **A hand copy in longhand, in the open, in a room with a second person in it, with his name and no office at the foot — and no box anywhere to put it in.** His hand is worse by the sixth bell and his right elbow is the elbow of Volume 01, and he cannot eat until the page is finished, which is a habit and not a virtue.

Continuity fact: **The chain is nine links and the figures do not close and are not meant to.** The batch's copy is not entered in anything. **Do not let the copy become evidence; the whole of Volume 01's lesson is that a hand copy made in breach of an order or outside a form is a second document and worth nothing in a room.**

Ending type: decision, cost, and an earned hook — because the next thing is not a copy, it is a form, and there is no form.

**Close of 60, four paragraphs:** Nim Rook comes and stands in the copy room because there has been nothing to carry for a month, and Marek tells her he has nothing for her, and she says the price was never the information, it was that he would say so, and that four people this month had better than a clerk's word. **The Trust's notice is still on the door downstairs and nobody has signed it. The public register is on the table by the window with a blank line in it and a signature written out in full. The seed goes in on the twelfth.**
