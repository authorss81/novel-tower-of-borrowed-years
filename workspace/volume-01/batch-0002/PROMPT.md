## Where the story is

MR 412, the fifteenth of the rains and the days after. Tallow Ward has water and the flood is easing. Tallow Ward also has a bill that nobody has looked at.

- The ward's peak-day delivery certification was never signed, so the ward's lawful water for the peak is a quantity in no document. The water arrived anyway, off a valve a Warden broke five days against a sealed order.
- The peak-night settlement came due and the chain walked four steps — ward, no surety, false-witnessed, the living certifier — to **Ondra Kelb**, twenty, three weeks in the post, who countersigned Hesper's entry in good faith with a pencil and lost the years of his right hand.
- **Marek** suspended that settlement for six bells on the authority of a remedy in a margin that names an office nobody holds, and told nobody. He is a clerk who has now done something and said it out loud in a stair and a lane, and there is no procedure in the building that lets him undo it.
- The hearing is **the fifteenth at the eighth bell, the Trust's third floor, Renata Vole presiding**, with the ward's copy of the levy and the file's copy to be compared in the room.
- **Sera Holt** has told Marek that a hold's toll comes out of a yearline, that a thing will show up in him within a month, and that it will not be dramatic.

## Locked instruments — never blur these

- **The ward settlement entry** is the false-witnessed instrument. Hesper signed it the ninth day; **Ondra Kelb countersigned it in pencil the same afternoon**. It carries the invented standing-reserve recital and the omission of page three from the ward's notice. The collection lands here.
- **The clerk's certificate of posting** is a separate one-page paper. **Marek signed it on the eleventh day** and wrote *Yes* on the posting desk's query that recorded him as a party. It made the settlement due at the peak. It is not the chain, it is not the false term, and Marek's name is never where the collection lands. It becomes the Chamber's instrument against him in Chapter 32 and the paper he surrenders in Chapter 44.
- **The chain follows the instrument, not the folder.** Say this in one line of procedure, not as a puzzle.
- **Page three** is the labour-pledge schedule: eleven lines, eleven sureties named, all eleven witness cells blank, never given to the ward. The ward's copy runs one, two, four, five.
- **The levy roll:** eleven named households, the posting names the first four, and **the Vale household is seventh of the eleven**. Marek's family pays later rather than sooner, which is how the posting looks survivable from his desk and is not.
- **Tomas's vigor loan is lawful, witnessed, and informed.** It is not the fraud, it is not on the roll, and it must never be blurred with it. The volume's moral target is the falsified entry, the invented reserve, and the chain — not a family making a hard bargain.
- The founding standing hold, the clerk of record, and the Trust's no-copy standing instruction are already canon. Read them in `bible/power-system.md` and `bible/terminology.md`; do not restate them as new.

## Power rules for this batch

- **Ledger Sight** may be used sparingly, at most one panel in the whole batch if you use one at all. It needs physical contact and a stressed hand, it reads visible entries, the witness chain and one immediate contradiction, and it **cannot produce a missing entry, a name nobody wrote, or an intention.** It never judges what is written. Its costs are real: headache, tunnel vision, and a small recent ordinary memory gone. Marek has used it five times in ten days and he is beginning to budget for the fee, which is its own kind of horror. Chapter 15 is the batch's practice chapter and it is a *hand-copy*, not a power use.
- **No second hold.** The standing hold is used once in the series and it is spent. Marek cannot do it again and must not try. Nothing above Boundary Hold. No Season Hand, no Returnpath, no Branch Auditor, no borrowed advantage.
- The delay is his and stays as a year scar. The collection was never his to direct.
- **Marek's own year scar from the hold should surface in this batch**, late, quietly, and not as a spectacle. Chapters 11 and 12 are the chapters that open on what a body pays, and his is now one of the bodies.

## What this batch must do

- Show the ward's real gratitude for a held settlement and its real hatred for the junior clerk the clerks are blaming. The ward is a community, not a file.
- Let **the Trust certify and post** the settlement anyway, and let **Hesper rewrite the file's cause as operator error.** Hesper is not a cartoon: he kept clinics open, he is astonished that his private arithmetic has a witness in it, and he becomes a monster only when he chooses a person to be the answer. That choice is Chapter 20.
- Give the batch one method chapter and one hand-copy chapter: **15** (Marek copies the substituted page longhand and learns what a hand-copy costs a body and an hour) and **19** (Marek and Neris run the ward's scar examination badly and then well). These are the two deliberate practice chapters of the volume. Play them as technique, not as service.
- **Pella Rusk** arrives in Chapter 18 with the school roll. Her name has been sitting in an unsigned surety cell since Chapter 4 and she does not know. Do not resolve that here; give her a reason to be angry at the levy that is hers alone, and let the two things sit beside each other.
- Do not let the romance advance past professional respect and one argument neither of them wins. No physical affection. No instant trust.
- Keep Lio ordinary and direct. Keep Hesper human. Keep Ilya a rule-breaker the ward is fed by and cannot yet thank.

## Chapter map (authoritative beats, from `outline/volume-01.md` Movement II)

**11 — The clinic.** What a body pays, what is already gone, and the only question left being whose. Marek is on the table or beside it. Open the batch here, on consequence, not on argument.
**12 — The ward at dawn.** Real gratitude for the held settlement, real hatred for the junior clerk the clerks are blaming. Aldis Kern does not trust anyone, including the clerks who just saved his ward.
**13 — Vole certifies and posts; Hesper rewrites cause as operator error.** The file acquires a second, kinder, false story.
**14 — Kelb's household and the counter-clerks' guild.** What it means to be the last name on a chain. Kelb is not written to forgive quickly.
**15 — Marek copies the substituted page by hand at night.** Nim Rook is the runner and states her price. Practice chapter: what a hand-copy costs.
**16 — Hesper's offer.** The family's settlement schedule cleared — the road work, not the years — if the copy stays in a drawer. This is the first time anyone pays him.
**17 — Tomas refuses help again.** A north-road crew member is hurt by borrowed vigor, and the vigor bill is real.
**18 — Pella Rusk brings the school roll.** The levy takes a school year from four Tallow children.
**19 — Neris's examination of the ward's scars.** The batch's method chapter, played badly and then well. The only chapter where Marek and Neris do real work together, and they still do not like each other.
**20 — Hesper's second certification.** It makes Kelb's countersignature the whole of the case. He now knows what the file is. This is where the volume's villainy starts, and it must be a decision, not a reveal.

## Length

Target roughly 24,000–28,000 words across ten chapters. Ordinary chapters **2,400–3,200 words**; 11, 15, 19 and 20 may run longer. Batch 0001 overran its word budget; do not repeat that. Never pad, and never cut a complete scene to hit a number.

## Panel budget

At most **one** Ledger Sight panel in the entire batch, and only if the scene cannot be done without it. Sparse ink, plain language, no percentages, no verdicts, no predictions, no quest markers, no menus. Chapters 11–20 carry no panel at all if you can avoid it.

## Guardrails

- Do not answer the missing year, name the Last Convocation, or explain A-0. Nobody in Morrowfen knows who A-0 is.
- Do not introduce any lender, market, broker, Choir, military body, or regional political crisis. Renata Vole is a Trust administrator. **Bel Harrow does not appear in this batch** and must not be foreshadowed as arriving.
- Do not make the hold cheap, aimed, or heroic. It was unauthorized, it was six bells, and it hurt a clerk who did not agree to it.
- Do not let Ledger Sight solve anything. It points; Marek still has to stand in the mud, ask difficult people, and choose.
- The ward roll naming district **4 — Kallow** was planted at the close of Chapter 10. It is not pursued here and must not recur in Movements II–V.
- Do not advance the clerk of record. Marek may describe himself as making a promise; nobody appoints him.
- End the batch on a completed cost and an earned hook, not an interruption mid-action.
