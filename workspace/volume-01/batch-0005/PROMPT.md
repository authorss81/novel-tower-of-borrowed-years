## Where the story is

MR 412, **the sixteenth of next month, a Thursday. The rain is not doing anything.** The board sits at the eighth bell in a room built for thirty. The recount has been held and the cause is in the desk's book. The collection is unblocked. Hesper Rann's authority to certify is not, and that is Thursday's problem.

- **The board** is five members: **Bel Harrow** presiding and on the list of members ineligible to be chair; **Renata Vole** for the Trust, who has already said in writing that this office does not intend to be delayed by a board; **Sedge** for the Chamber; **Aldis Kern** for the ward in his own name; **Zorka Vendl** for the Benevolent Fund, who was asked twice to take money and declined twice. **The Chair does not vote in a money matter.** Neris Kade has the second seat and a sheet. **Harrow serves the minutes himself in his own hand, because no office in the city will serve the record of a body it is a party to.**
- **The four terms are agreed and written.** The recorder's rate is carried by the Trust because a ward cannot be assessed one. A party may decline to answer and the declining is written down and no inference is drawn. **A member who dissents may have the grounds written in their own words, in the finding, and the duty to write them is the board's and not the member's** — Vole refused this on the record twice in the same words. Plus Harrow's own term, in a different hand: **no collection is entered while the board is open**, which is four days and a queue behind a door and not a stay, and **nobody moved against it and Hesper Rann's not moving against it is the tell.**
- **The board's first act has been taken: it recorded all four figures and adjudicated none of them.** 640, 440, 279h 40p, 441. It must not choose one.
- **About three hundred and forty ward people are outside the room.** Nine are in it, all persons in their own names, because **a ward is not a person and a room holds thirty and cannot be cross-examined, sworn or served.** The Trust's motion that the ward's attendance is not required failed on the ninth. **Chapters 41–45 need the ward's families seated and the Trust's not, and the way to do that is a room that holds thirty and a rule about who is a party.**
- **Kelb's name is in a desk's book under a seal in a cause line and in a hundred mouths, and he has not been asked for anything.** His mother stood up in a room on the twelfth and said his name twice and said nobody had looked at the boy. He has not been able to ask anybody what to call a column for three weeks and has not gone and asked.
- **The ward's own record is three boards on a wall in the Gate yard in forty hands** and none of them can be carried into a room: the roll of the pail water, the oiled sheet of the forty items of the fourteenth with the Underbank at the top of it, and the two sheets of twenty-nine answers with three no's.

- **The school is shut** — it reopened on the first for the week the Ch 13 record bought and closed again on the sixth — and **the school is worth fourteen places against ninety-one children**, a number Pella Rusk has said once, in a yard, and will say again only in a room.

## A ward is not a person — the line that carries Chapter 41

Stated once, at the door of the Threshold on the twenty-second, in Chapter 21: a ward **cannot petition, cannot appeal, cannot be sworn, and cannot be cross-examined**, because a ward cannot speak; every form in the hall has a box for a person in it and **there is no box for Tallow Ward and there has never been one.** Nine people in Chapter 39 got into a room by being persons. **Chapter 41 needs the same trick and the same rule, and the rule is the obstacle and the engine at once.** Do not restate the whole rule again. Two people can speak for the ward: the clerk of the day, who is a party and is **suspended**, and Kern, in his own name, alone. **A suspended clerk is also a party and is a convenience for nobody.** Make that cost something in the room.
