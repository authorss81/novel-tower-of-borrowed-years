## What the volume was about

A sequence loan **requested in writing by the fourth terrace of the Red Uplands, with reasons, and sealed in the hills in MR 412**, favoured the basin's season. Its counterweight is named as a class — **the seed-keepers of the four terraces after this season** — in nine words of type with a hand-filled number in front of them, so **nobody was ever asked.** A Crown March granary office advanced money against a projection of that season's yield, sold the paper on, and the sheet carried a seal and a Tide Reckoning mark before anybody had written a word on it.

**The instrument is well made and no chapter of this volume found a defect in it, exactly as no chapter of Volume 02 found a defect in the Share's instrument. **The volume's question was *why are signatures being copied across unrelated contracts*, and its answer is institutional and partial.** A Tide Reckoning mark is four parts and no day — a month, a tide phase, a harbour seal, a translator — so **a period can be put on any day inside it**, checking it needs a tide table, and there is one in this basin in two rooms above a chandler's shop.
