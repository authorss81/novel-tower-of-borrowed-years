## Where the story is

The kept-back went into nine hundred and forty acres on the nineteenth of the growth month, well, and pleased nobody. **Eleven of sixty-one germination trays failed off the oldest kept-back stock in the basin, out of a shed shut up wet in the flood year, and one of the eleven is Wenna Drel's, and a shower went over the ridge on the ninth and missed the low ground by four miles, and a ditch on the low ground has had a rate on each side of it since a sluice was cut into the high side twenty-two years back, and the district's flood quarters were restated on the eighteenth on a correct rule that cost four households a fortnight.
