## The volume in one paragraph

A sequence loan requested by the fourth terrace of the Red Uplands and sealed in the hills in MR 412 favoured the basin's season, and its counterweight is named as a class — *the seed-keepers of the four terraces after this season* — on a printed form, with the words printed and only the number filled in by hand, so nobody was ever asked. **The present consequence is the best harvest in nine years: the low ground's rate falls by about a third in nine days, forty carts a night come down off the uplands, the granary offices of the basin are content, and the town has been eating since before the harvest month.** Underneath it, nine lots out of thirty-nine on the second terrace have come up as sand, the third terrace has stopped reporting, and a Crown March granary office has advanced **eleven thousand and some hundreds of pounds** against a projection of those terraces' future yield and sold the paper on. **The security is a future famine being used as collateral in the only direction available: the buyer is assured the district will be able to deliver later, because later there will be grain.** A granary official signs the clause, and the mark in its corner is a Tide Reckoning mark — four parts, of which none is a day — and the same hand is on a Morrowfen bundle, on a board over a shed door, on a haulier's contract and on the lid of a crate of forms in a cellar, **and nobody in Morrowfen can read one, and a mark that names a month can go on any day inside that month.** The register of risk is six weeks live in a shed in Tallow Ward and has just refused a paper whose counterweight is a class, not because anybody ruled but because there is nobody in the room who is the person behind it.

## The world, for a writer who has not read any of Volume 03

- **Morrowfen, MR 413 into MR 414.** The Yearspire is the city's bank, courthouse, emergency office, archive and power plant. **Tallow Ward** is kitchens, tanneries, fuel yards and manual labour, four hundred and six people, the Gate tank, the trestle, the shed, and the register. **Rainmarket** is seed, grain, river trade, the low ground, the weighbridge, the river stair and the Elmsworth seed house. **Bell Stair** is the administrative hill: the Chamber, the copy room, the posting desk, and a cellar under a chandler's yard. **The Red Uplands** are four terraces and a stone bridge and a road with eleven switchbacks that takes a day, and **Hartlow is the fourth and the top.** **Crown March** is the regional administrative heartland, and its granary depots are at **Verrow.**
- **A loan has five parts** — need, pledge, scope, witness, settlement. **A sequence loan's counterweight may be named as a district, or as a class, and neither is a person, and there has never been a form on which either consents.** In this volume the counterweight is a class **printed on a form**, eleven times, and that is new.
- **A Tide Reckoning mark is four parts and no day: a month, a tide phase, a harbour seal, a translator.** It is the only mark in this basin that names a period rather than a point, so it can be put on any day inside that month, and checking it requires a tide table. **A refusal to swear is not a denial. Nobody in Morrowfen can read one. An audit in this city goes as far as the seal and stops.**
- **A projection is a granary's forward estimate of a yield, entered in the year before it.** An advance is money paid early against one. An underwriting clause assures a buyer that a district will deliver. **The depot enters a district at the ten-year mean and is not permitted to produce its own estimate, because the moment it can, the estimate is an offer instead of a measurement and an offer can be sued.**
- **A register of risk is not a roll, not a return and not a schedule.** A person may enter a line about their own paper and **nobody may enter a line about anybody else's**; the first question at the counter is *is that your line, is that the person behind it, is it your words*; a bundle may not be entered as one line; nobody may carry a paper out; **and a paper whose person behind it is not in the room is not entered at all.** Its fifth line is the hours and is empty because the hours are nobody's paper; its sixth line is the days and is empty for ever.
- **A store room with a key has a keyholder, and a keyholder is a person, and a person can be asked.** **Nobody can be sent to ask, and there is no form in this kingdom in which anybody may be applied for.** That is the ninth line of the season works and it is why the season is the volume's clock and not the law.
- **A ward is not a person and a terrace is not a district and a holding is not a household and a class is not a person.** A figure about any of them cannot be entered, and if it could it could be added up.
- **Nobody in Morrowfen knows who A-0 is.** **The Last Convocation, the First Account, the crossing year, the missing year and Edren Sable have not been mentioned once in one hundred and ten chapters.**
