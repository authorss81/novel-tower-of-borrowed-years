## The volume in one paragraph

Volume 03's plant is cashed in a form. **A mark that names a month can be put on any day inside it; a form that names a line can be put on any day inside a person's life, and the form is older than the Trust and is still the form in use.** A lifelong attestation is four ruled lines: a name, a line that is never a paper, a printed promise to speak to **any paper touching that line while the signer lives**, and a date that is a month and not a day. **There is no line on the form that names the paper.** It was shortened nineteen years ago in a print shop in Bell Stair to save a quarter of a sheet on four hundred copies, by a woman of twenty-four who has thought about it every working day since, and the cutting of one line of type is why about nine people in this basin have been told in four months that they are witnesses to things they signed nothing about, and why each of them can now be produced as a witness exactly once and never again. **A record loan of the eleventh of the third month, sealed lawfully to stop a district account being struck out, paid for the change in what the institution would accept with fourteen attestations, and a class of people's willingness cannot consent.** Nobody forged anything. There is no forger and there is nobody to be angry with and there is no form on which a person may be entered as saying they did not agree. And on the last night of the ninth month of MR 415, in a room eight feet by eleven behind the Threshold, a clerk who has nothing but his own word counter-signed a certified copy he had not read the page behind, to give a woman of twenty-two a document instead of a grievance — **and the document is good, and he is spent, and no office in this kingdom can give it back.**
