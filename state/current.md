## The volume in one paragraph

A reckoning year is ten months long and the year turns on the last night of the ninth month, and the month after that is the first month of the new year, and a form that names a **line** can be put on any day inside that line's life, and there is no box anywhere in this kingdom on which a person may be entered as saying they were free when they signed anything, and there never has been one, and it is not going to be one. **Nobody forged anything.** A woman of twenty-four cut a line of type out of a forme nineteen years ago to save a quarter of a sheet on about four hundred copies, a clerk of the Chamber put it back on the fourth of the fifth month of MR 416 in a copy room in Bell Stair and said nothing about whether it was right, and the line is two words and a blank, and **a blank with a head on it is worse than the form it replaced, because the paper it wants named does not exist when the paper is signed, and the price of filling it in is that every witness in this kingdom is spent on the day they stand up.** A hearing officer read four documents out on a causeway with the reason each is dangerous and with no person on them, and voided one and confirmed one and could not touch two, and a hearing officer's own name is now on a counter's page. **Three rooms sat on three days without knowing about each other and their three findings make a quorum that no office can be shown and no person can be compelled to, and the quorum is nothing but a place where a man could be stopped and not be charged for it.** A settlement marshal was answered by a table of paper and not by a person, and is right about the water, and is not destroyed, and does not repent, and was not given the word she asked for a fourth time. A name was read aloud in a room and the other half of the page is on an address that no office in this kingdom can be shown. **And a form with a line missing is the only criminal this volume had, and the line is back in it, and the fixing of it moved the harm from a piece of ground to a person, and nothing was made safe.**
