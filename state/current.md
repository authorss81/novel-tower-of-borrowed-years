## The volume in one paragraph

A rain-basin seed cooperative — the Elmsworth Seed Share, sixty-one smallholders who pledged their own labour and a third of their own seed to keep a seed house open through the bad autumn of MR 411 — is one short of a harvest it cannot lose, and the Sable Compact's Morrowfen house will sell the Share's paper and its members' district-rate arrears as a single bundled instrument at a price smallholders can afford. The Trust's credit window for the season has closed. Every signature in the stack is permitted by a rule that predates the Compact, every figure balances to the penny, and nothing anybody signs is a lie. The Share's own fourth clause names a **counterweight as a district**, and so does **the Trust's own printed form, in the fourth box, in type, and has for nineteen years**; a district cannot be asked anything; when the low ground's rate is charged, the ratepayers in arrears assign their collection rights to the Compact at a discount, the Compact sells the paper, and the chain walks nine links out of Rainmarket and ends on one household's arrears in Tallow Ward in the ninth month — the same month the ward's own reserve carry comes due. Movement IV built the counter-move: a **register of risk in a shed in Tallow Ward in which a bundle may not be entered as one line**, paid for out of a levy of one hundred and forty a season agreed by a cooperative and a ward that cannot be assessed a rate at all. It is a practice with hours on it and it is not a law, and it is not live until Chapter 95, and it cannot enter the paper it was built to beat.
