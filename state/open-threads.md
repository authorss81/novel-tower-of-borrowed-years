## Guardrails for every batch

- One slow-burn relationship (Marek and Neris). No harem, no instant resolution, no using their bond as a consent token.
- System panels are rare, in-world, and short. No percentages, quest markers, verdicts, or predictions.
- Power comes from practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. A new option or a new responsibility, not a bigger effect.
- A completed loan is not automatically a tragedy. The question is whether the cost was told, refusable, and still treated as permanent.
- The ending is fixed: the tower is transformed, the founding claim is released by disclosure, and no one is resurrected or made all-powerful.

Tomas still refuses it on its own terms, and Chapter 48 is where the settlement schedule becomes supervised road work.**
- **Hesper's sister, forty-four, twenty-two years on a copying floor on the fifth floor of the fund building, home at a quarter to six.** Eleven weeks ago a wounded man's wrist left a mark on the underside of a board when the wax came off, and she washed it off and told nobody, and has not mentioned it to her brother once. Hesper used it as a knife in Ch 38 and never said what the mark is. **Open, and it is not a year-scar thread: do not decode it, and do not let a character connect it to Marek's.**
- **Hesper has read the school register at the Tallow Gate and named Lio in it.** Off the twenty-fourth, back on the first for the week the Ch 13 record bought, off again on the sixth. He says the school is the last line on an order with no copy and the ward's levy days have not been drawn, and that when they are drawn that is a line above the school. **Open, and it is the personal cost of the cut order and the conversion.**
- **Tamsin's house book, and the struck line.** Five domestic columns, the fifth headed *carried*; in the fourth a ruled line with **four notches**; in the second a name in a different ink; and the whole line struck through in a third ink by a hand that is neither, with no account of the striking anywhere in the book. She says the number is not an accident and refuses to say whose it is, and says aloud that Marek is not going to be allowed to say the line was struck out by somebody with no authority. **He does not put it in his book and makes himself be clear that this is a decision and not an oversight. STILL OPEN. The account is Volume 16's; the public testimony is MR 435's. Do not explain the number and do not let a second person decode it.**
- **Kelb's schedule, and the column with nothing at the head of it.** Three rules he wrote himself, four columns, and a fifth column headed *what I am for now* with seven words in four days and no heading. He put his hand over it and said Marek may look again next Saturday and that he does not want the name if it arrives too easily, because he wants to see whether Marek goes and asks anybody. **Marek did not go and ask anybody.** **Open. The third pile's column heading has now had a fifth answer in nineteen hands on the board at the Gate and is still blank, and the yard has voted against filling it.** Named for a later volume; leaned on again in Ch 37 and available in Ch 46.
- **The hand-book, and the wrong hand.** Vendl asked for the Ropewalk maker by name, which was Hesper's kindness of Ch 20, and the apparatus works perfectly on the left. **Kelb's own left hand has taken over fine work it was never trained to and it is a nine-year-old's hand and it is better than the hand-book and it is not his.** The experiment cost him four dead days, his mother's basket, and one of his own rules broken by his own hand. **Open, and Ch 49 is his restitution.**
- **The board cannot do three things, and each of them is a wall for Chapter 41.** It cannot strike an entry out of a desk's book; it cannot choose between the four figures; and it cannot serve a ward, because a ward is not a person. A notice of a hearing therefore is not a service but a thing a man in a good coat does at a tank, and **a room built for thirty is the whole of the ward's standing in the proceeding that decides its bill.** The four hundred will be in a corridor and a stair in the rain on the sixteenth, and the first thing said in the room will have to be said by a person with a name in a box.
- **Marek's second reason to be in the room, and it is not a form.** Three boards on a wall in the Gate yard in forty hands — the yard roll of the pail water, the oiled sheet of the forty items of the fourteenth with the Underbank at the top of it, and now the two sheets of twenty-nine answers with three no's — none of which can be carried into a room, and a wet notice of the hearing above all three. **He has four things in his book and none of them is worth anything, and a fifth in a house book that is not his to give away.** **Open, and it is the reason he is in the room on the sixteenth without a form.**
