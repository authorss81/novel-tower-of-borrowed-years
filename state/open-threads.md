# Open Threads

Threads are tracked here so a batch never drops one. Each entry names the owner volume. Do not resolve a thread early and do not open a new major canon rule without recording it in the bible and in `state/continuity.md`.

## Reader-facing mystery

- **Why does every ledger begin with the same missing year, and why does the oldest reserve hold consequences from a future that no longer exists?** Owner: the full arc, answered in Volume 18. Ladder: Volume 1 blank first entry and a missing ward on the map; 2–4 the entry is not clerical error; 5–7 people are removed, not just names; 8 the Last Meridian is named; 11 the Last Convocation and Edren's copied authority, crossing year still sealed; 13 the First Account, the Founder's Clause, and the crossing-year entry are identified; 15 the crossing year's **mechanism** and the price of restoring it; 18 Movement A its **human cost**, and the climax ending the claim by disclosure rather than recovery. Intentionally open afterward: how the natural Yearwell formed, whether the Last Meridian's unreachable futures exist as places, whether a few isolated year echoes can be recovered, and what becomes of every individual erased name.
- **Who is A-0?** Seeded in Volume 1 as a signature clerks are forbidden to erase. Volume 10 poses the question—who is A-0, and what authority does the signature claim over the archive. Volume 11 names the title and puts Edren in the room. His authority is fully explained in Volumes 13 and 15.

## Guardrails for every batch

- One slow-burn relationship (Marek and Neris). No harem, no instant resolution, no using their bond as a consent token.
- System panels are rare, in-world, and short. No percentages, quest markers, verdicts, or predictions.
- Power comes from practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. A new option or a new responsibility, not a bigger effect.
- A completed loan is not automatically a tragedy. The question is whether the cost was told, refusable, and still treated as permanent.
- The ending is fixed: the tower is transformed, the founding claim is released by disclosure, and no one is resurrected or made all-powerful.
